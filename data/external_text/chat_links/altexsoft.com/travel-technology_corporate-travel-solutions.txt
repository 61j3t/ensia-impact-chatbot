OBT (Online Booking Tool) Development
Self-service online booking tools allow travelers to book trips independently while following company policies. Such tools consider traveler profiles and connect with HR, finance, ERP, and other systems for better data exchange and reporting. We will build or customize an OBT to streamline your travel reservation processes and policy compliance.
