About this policy
Our Website uses cookies. This policy (“Cookie Policy”) provides you information about the cookies used on this Website, why AltexSoft Inc. (“AltexSoft”, “we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies, and how you can manage the cookies used by this Website.
By using this Website, you are consenting to our use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy. If you do not agree to our use of cookies in this way, you should set your browser settings accordingly or not use the Website. If you disable cookies that we use, this may impact your user experience while on our Website.
What are cookies?
Cookies are small text files, frequently with a singular identifier, that are placed on your device or browser when you visit our Website or any other web address. Cookies are then sent back to the originating web address on each subsequent visit, or to another web address that recognizes that cookie.
Types of cookies
Depending on who sets the cookies on your device or browser, the cookies can be divided into first-party and third-party cookies:
- First-party cookies are cookies set by our Website when you’re visiting it. Only our Website can read them and store information collected by such cookies.
- Third-party cookies are cookies set by the external services we might use on our Website.
Some third-party services that we use to improve the performance of our Website may place their own cookies in your browser. This Cookie Policy covers the use of cookies by AltexSoft only and not the use of cookies by third parties.
To learn more about third-party service providers who may have the access to your personal information via cookies, please read our Privacy Policy
Since the installation of third-party cookies and other tracking systems through the services used within AltexSoft cannot be technically controlled by us, any specific references to cookies and tracking systems installed by third parties are to be considered indicative. To obtain complete information, we kindly ask you to consult the privacy policies of the respective third-party services. Given the objective complexity surrounding the identification of technologies based on cookies, we encourage you to contact us should you wish to receive any further information on the use of cookies by AltexSoft.
Duration-wise, there are:
- session cookies which are deleted when you quit your browser and are usually used to allow our Website to link your actions during a browser session, and
- persistent cookies which are saved on your device in between browser sessions and that are not deleted automatically when you quit your browser. Persistent cookies are used for a variety of purposes including remembering your preferences and choices when using our Website or to target advertising.
All persistent Cookies usually have an expiration date (no longer than 12 months), after which they are automatically removed from your device. Anonymized and aggregated data can be stored for an indefinite period of time by us for analysis purposes.
Why do we use cookies?
Cookies allow our Website to provide you with a customized experience. Depending on the purpose of each cookie, cookies enable our Website to work as intended or work more efficiently, to tailor our Website to your personal needs, to improve its user-friendliness and to communicate to you elsewhere on the web. Some cookies allow us to get the statistics in an anonymized and aggregated form on how you use our Website so that we can improve it and enhance your browsing experience.
Cookies do lots of different tasks, like remembering your browsing preferences, letting you navigate between pages efficiently and smoothly, and generally improving your user experience. Cookies may tell us, for example, whether you have used the website before or whether you are a new user.
These are cookies that are set on our Website by our partner companies (e.g. Facebook, Quora, LinkedIn, YouTube or other third parties). They may use the data collected from these cookies to anonymously target advertising to you on other websites, based on your behavior to this Website.

How to control or delete cookies
When you visit our website, we ask your permission to use cookies. If you do not agree to accept them, we will use only essential cookies, that allow our Website to work properly. Continuing to navigate on this Website also means you accept the use of those cookies.
Functional, analytics, advertisement, and/or other cookies on our Website will be used for your visit only provided that you gave us your explicit consent to use them.
Also, if you gave such consent, you can freely withdraw it at any time following the instructions provided in Do not track preferences. Please note: this feature will opt you out (or in) of cookies too. Also, if you choose to opt-out, you will receive an opt-out cookie (ignore) so that your decision is remembered. If you regularly delete your cookies, you will need to opt-out each time you delete them.
Please note that if you choose to refuse cookies, you may not be able to fulfill some functions or purposes on this Website, like verifying login status, protecting security of your login.
If you have disabled one or more cookies, we may still use information collected from cookies prior to your disabled preference being set, however, we will stop using the disabled cookie to collect any further information.
Do not track preferences
Do not track is a function that allows you to opt out from being tracked by websites for any purpose including the use of analytics services, advertising networks and social platforms.
You can update your preferences or withdraw your consent at any time – most devices (in the case of mobile applications) and browsers (in the case of web apps and pages) allow you to change your cookie settings. These settings will typically be found in the “options” or “preferences” menu of your browser.
Most browsers and devices allow you to:
- see what cookies you’ve got and delete them on an individual basis;
- block third-party cookies;
- block cookies from particular websites;
- block all cookies from being set;
- delete all cookies when you quit your browser.
Through browser preferences, it is also possible to delete cookies installed in the past, including the cookies that may have saved the initial consent for the installation of cookies by this Website. If you chose to delete cookies, you should be aware that any preferences will be lost. Also, if you block cookies completely, many websites (including ours) might not work properly.
The following links provide information about how to manage cookies on popular browsers, otherwise you should use the “Help” option in your browser for more details.
To find information relating to other browsers, visit the browser developer’s website. If you have any questions about changing preferences, email us at legal@altexsoft.com.
You may also follow the instructions provided on the initiatives by the EDAA (EU), the Network Advertising Initiative (US) and the Digital Advertising Alliance (US), DAAC (Canada), DDAI (Japan) or other similar services. Such initiatives may allow you to select your tracking preferences for most of the advertising tools.
Our cookies are not used to identify you personally. They’re just there to make our Website work better for you.
