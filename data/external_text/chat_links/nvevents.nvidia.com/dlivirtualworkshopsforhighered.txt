Join NVIDIA’s Deep Learning Institute (DLI) for a free, virtual instructor-led workshop providing hands-on experience with GPU-accelerated servers in the cloud to complete end-to-end projects in the areas of deep learning, accelerated computing, and accelerated data science. Each of these workshops are led by a DLI Certified Instructor and offer an opportunity to earn an industry-recognized certificate of competency based on assessments to support your career growth. All workshops are delivered remotely in a virtual environment.
Prerequisites
- Only current students, researchers and faculty with a valid email address from a college or university in Europe, Israel, Middle East or Africa are eligible to attend. You will only be able to register for one of these workshops.
