Your Voice Matters
Before we begin, we’d like to hear from you. This quick survey is designed to help us understand the real experiences of students on campus. Your perspective is important, and every answer helps us build a clearer picture of student well-being.
Why we’re asking these questions
We want to better understand how students are feeling—physically, mentally, and emotionally. Your responses are completely anonymous and will help us identify the most important well-being issues students face on campus.
By answering honestly, you’re directly contributing to meaningful improvements and helping shape solutions that support the entire student community. Thank you for taking a few minutes to share your experience.
