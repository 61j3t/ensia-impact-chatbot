This workshop, presented by Xanadu and the Africa Quantum Consortium (AQC), focuses on the practical integration of quantum circuits into machine learning pipelines using PennyLane. While PennyLane now supports advanced algorithms like QSVT and resource estimation, this session is tailored specifically for those interested in Quantum Machine Learning (QML).
What You Will Learn:
PennyLane Introduction: A brief guide to the leading library for differentiable programming and quantum research.
Hybrid Architectures: How to treat quantum circuits as differentiable nodes within standard ML workflows.
Fourier Interpretations: A sample demonstration of how Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQAs) can be interpreted in terms of Fourier series.
Advanced Directions: A glimpse into the role of Fourier analysis in modern QML research, with curated resources for further study.
