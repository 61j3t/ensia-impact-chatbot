UTokyo Amgen Scholars Program 2026 Details:

- Funded By: Amgen Foundation
- Study Level: Undergraduate
- Eligibility: International students

- Free accommodation
- A stipend of 270,000 JPY for living expenses.
- Japanese cultural events and short trips.
- Student seminars and sessions.
- Hands-on research in state-of-the-art labs at the University of Tokyo.

Eligibility Criteria for the UTokyo Amgen Scholars Program 2026:
- Open to undergraduate students from any nationality.
- Applicants must be currently enrolled in a university or have recently graduated.
- Must have finished 1 year of studies before the program starts.
- Strong academic performance is required, focusing on biotechnology or life sciences.
- Applicants should be at least 18 years old by the program start date.
- Proficiency in English is essential; non-native speakers may need to provide language test scores.
- Commitment to the full duration of the program is necessary.
- English Proficiency (for non-native speakers): TOEFL iBT 72, IELTS 5.5, Cambridge English FCE, TOEIC 1095, TOEIC L&R 785, or TOEIC S&W 310.
Required Documents:
- Completed application form.
- Personal Identification
- Academic transcripts.
- Curriculum Vitae (CV).
- Proof of English proficiency.
- Any additional documents specified by the program.
