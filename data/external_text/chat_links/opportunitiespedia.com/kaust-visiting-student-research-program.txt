Eligibility Criteria for the KAUST Visting Student Research Program 2026:
- Open to students of all nationalities.
- Open to undergraduate students in their 3rd year or higher, as well as master’s students.
- A minimum GPA of 3.5 out of 4 is required.
- Strong verbal and written English proficiency (no test scores required).
- Current PhD candidates, KAUST students, and KAUST alumni are not eligible to apply.

How to apply for the KAUST International Internship 2026?
- Visit the KAUST VSRP website: Go to the KAUST VSRP website and review the eligibility requirements, program details, and application process.
- Create an account: Create an account on the KAUST VSRP online application portal and fill out the required information, including personal and academic details.
- Upload required documents: Upload all the required documents, such as your academic transcripts, CV, personal statement, and two letters of recommendation.
- Choose research areas: Select the research areas that interest you the most and align with your academic background and career aspirations.
- Submit your application: Submit your completed application before the application deadline.
- Wait for the selection process: The selection committee will review your application and notify you of their decision within a few weeks.
