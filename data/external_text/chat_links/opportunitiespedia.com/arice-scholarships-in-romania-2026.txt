The Romanian ARICE Scholarships 2026-2027 offer an excellent fully funded opportunity for international students to pursue higher education in Europe. Offered by the Romanian Agency for Investments and Foreign Trade in collaboration with the Ministry of Education in Romania, the program provides 40 scholarships annually for foreign students wishing to study in Romania.
These scholarships support students pursuing Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD degrees at accredited Romanian state universities. The initiative aims to strengthen international educational cooperation while promoting the Romanian language and culture among global scholars.
One of the key highlights of the scholarship is the free Romanian language preparatory year, allowing students to adapt academically and culturally before beginning their degree programs. Through this scholarship, students can experience high-quality education, rich cultural heritage, and vibrant student life in Romania. Romanian ARICE Scholarships 2026-2027 Details:
- Host Country: Romania
- Offered By: Romanian Agency for Investments and Foreign Trade
- Study Levels: Bachelor’s, Master’s, PhD
- Eligibility: International Students (Non-EU & Non-EFTA)
Benefits of Romanian ARICE Scholarships 2026-2027:
- No application or registration fees
- Free one-year Romanian language preparatory course
- Full tuition fee coverage
- Monthly living stipend
- Free accommodation in university dormitories
- Free medical assistance
- Free local public transportation, ground transport
- Rail transport
- Underground transport
- Naval domestic transport
Note: International airfare and airport transfers are not covered.

Eligibility Criteria for Romanian ARICE Scholarships 2026-2027:
- Open to non-EU and non-EFTA citizens
- Priority given to applicants from countries without educational or cultural agreements with Romania
- Must demonstrate strong academic performance
- Must meet university admission requirements
- Must either:
- Know Romanian, or
- Complete the free preparatory language year
- Academic qualifications must be equivalent to Romanian university standards
Required Documents:
- Academic transcripts and certificates
- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Recommendation letters
- Language proficiency proof (if available)
- Passport copy
- Completed scholarship application form
Deadline:
The application deadline to apply for the Romanian ARICE Scholarships is 12 June 2026.

How to Apply for the Romanian ARICE Scholarships 2026-2027?
Follow these steps to apply:
- Check Eligibility: Ensure you meet nationality and academic requirements.
- Choose a Program: Select a study program at a Romanian public university.
- Prepare Documents: Gather all required academic and personal documents.
