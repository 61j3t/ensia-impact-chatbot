DESY Summer Student Program 2026 Details:
- Host Country: Germany
- Host: The Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron (DESY)
- Program Dates: 21 July – 10 September 2026
- Eligibility: International Students

- 1200 euros monthly allowance for all participants.
- 850 euros for students from Hamburg and nearby areas.

Eligibility Criteria for the DESY Summer Students Program 2026:
- Open to international students from all countries.
- A strong command of English is required for participation in lectures and seminars.
- Full commitment to the program is required from start to finish.
- Must have completed three years of full-time studies in physics, computing, or related disciplines.
- Applicants who have obtained a Bachelor’s degree or are currently studying for a Master’s degree are eligible.
- Previous participants of the DESY Summer Program are not eligible.
Required Documents:
- CV/Resume
- Academic Transcript
- Letter of Motivation
- Proof of English proficiency
