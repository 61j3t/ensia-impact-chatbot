Eligibility Criteria for the HIT Chinese Government Scholarship 2026:
- Open to all international and non-Chinese candidates.
- For the master’s program, a bachelor’s degree is required.
- For the doctoral program, a master’s degree is required.
- The age limit for the master’s program is under 35 years.
- The age limit for the doctoral program is under 40 years.
- Applicants should demonstrate good research ability.
- A strong academic record is expected.
Required Documents:
- Completed application form.
- Notarized highest degree certificate.
- Notarized academic transcripts.
- Study or research plan (800 words).
- Two recommendation letters.
- English Proficiency Test Certificate
- Passport copy.
- Physical examination form.
Deadline:
The application deadline to apply for the Harbin Institute of Technology Scholarships 2026 for International Students in China is February 20, 2026.

How to apply for the Harbin Institute of Technology CSC Scholarship 2026?
- Complete the CSC Application: Register on the CSC portal (https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn), select “Type B” for HIT, and fill in the required information. Submit and download the application form.
- Apply on HIT’s Portal: Visit the HIT application portal, fill in the online form, upload the required documents, and submit.
- Submit Documents: Ensure you upload all necessary documents (e.g., degree certificates, transcripts, study plan) on both the CSC and HIT portals.
- Await Results: HIT will review applications, and successful candidates will be contacted via email.
