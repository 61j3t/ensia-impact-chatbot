To qualify for admission to Norwegian universities without IELTS in 2026, applicants can often submit an English Proficiency Certificate showing they completed a degree taught in English. Alternative English Tests to Study in Norway without IELTS 2026:
- TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) – Many universities in Norway accept the TOEFL iBT as an alternative to IELTS. A typical minimum score is around 80-90, depending on the program and institution.
- PTE Academic (Pearson Test of English) – Some Norwegian universities also accept PTE Academic, with a minimum score requirement typically ranging from 50 to 63 for most programs.
- Cambridge English Exams – Certificates such as the Cambridge C1 Advanced (CAE) and C2 Proficiency (CPE) are widely accepted as IELTS alternatives. Scores vary by university, but a minimum level of C1 or C2 is often required.
- Duolingo English Test – This online test is increasingly accepted by universities globally, including some in Norway. Score requirements vary, but a minimum score of 105-120 is typically required.
- English-Taught Previous Education – If your previous degree (bachelor’s or master’s) was taught in English, many Norwegian institutions may waive the English proficiency requirement. You would need to provide proof, such as a certificate from your previous institution stating that English was the medium of instruction.
- Native Language Exception – Some universities exempt students from English proficiency tests if they come from countries where English is the official language.
