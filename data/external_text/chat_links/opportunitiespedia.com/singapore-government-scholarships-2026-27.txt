The Singapore Government Scholarships 2026-2027 offer an excellent opportunity for international students, especially those from ASEAN countries, to pursue secondary or undergraduate education in one of Asia’s most developed and innovation-driven countries. These prestigious scholarships, funded by the Ministry of Education (MOE) and other official bodies in Singapore, are designed to empower talented individuals through world-class education, leadership training, and global exposure.
Students can apply for scholarships ranging from ASEAN Secondary Scholarships to ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarships, and even the elite Dr Goh Keng Swee Scholarship funded by the Association of Banks in Singapore. These programs ensure full financial coverage and comprehensive support throughout the academic journey.

Major Scholarship Programs in Singapore:
1. ASEAN Scholarships (Secondary Level)
Administered by: Ministry of Education (MOE), Singapore
For: Students aged 14–16 from ASEAN countries
Purpose: Study in Singapore leading to GCE ‘A’ Level Certificate
2. ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship (AUS)
Funded by: Ministry of Education, Singapore
For: ASEAN nationals currently in 1st or 2nd year of undergraduate studies in their home country
Host Universities:
- National University of Singapore (NUS)
- Nanyang Technological University (NTU)
- Singapore Management University (SMU)
How It Works:
- Apply for admission to NUS, NTU, or SMU.
- If selected, apply for the ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship through the university.
3. Dr Goh Keng Swee Scholarship
Administered by: Association of Banks in Singapore (ABS)
For: Outstanding students from Asia (including Laos)
Covers:
- Full tuition fees
- Return airfare
- Annual maintenance allowance
- Hostel accommodation
Eligibility Criteria for Singapore Government Scholarships:
- Open to students from ASEAN countries (including Laos).
- Must demonstrate excellent academic performance.
- Strong leadership potential and active participation in extracurricular activities.
- Proficiency in English (some programs require English language tests).
- Meet age or enrollment requirements specified in each scholarship type.
Required Documents:
- Completed online application form
- Copy of passport or national ID
- Academic transcripts and certificates
- Proof of English language proficiency (if applicable)
- Personal statement or motivation letter
- Letters of recommendation
- Curriculum Vitae (CV)

Deadline:
- ASEAN Scholarships (Secondary): Application opens around March-May.
- ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarships: Depends on university application timeline (typically October – March )
- Dr Goh Keng Swee Scholarship: Usually opens in September, deadline around March.
Check respective official websites regularly for updates.
How to Apply for Singapore Government Scholarships 2026-2027?
- Select the Scholarship Type: Choose from ASEAN Secondary, ASEAN Undergraduate, or Dr Goh Keng Swee Scholarships.
- Visit Official Website: Go to the MOE or respective university/organization’s scholarship portal.
- Complete Online Application: Fill out personal, academic, and extracurricular details.
- Upload Required Documents: Submit scanned copies of certificates, transcripts, passport, etc.
- Submit Before Deadline: Ensure timely submission to avoid disqualification.
- Wait for Shortlisting: Shortlisted candidates may be invited for interviews or tests.
