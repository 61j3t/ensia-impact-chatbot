The RIFS Fellow Program 2027 offers a prestigious opportunity for researchers, professionals, artists, policymakers, and changemakers from around the world to contribute to sustainability transformations. Hosted by the Research Institute for Sustainability at the GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences in Potsdam, Germany, this international fellowship supports innovative, interdisciplinary projects that address global sustainability challenges.
The fellowship program is one of the defining features of RIFS, bringing together approximately ten fellows each year from diverse sectors of society. Fellows collaborate closely with RIFS research groups, develop innovative ideas, and engage in transformative research aimed at advancing sustainable futures.
The RIFS Fellow Program 2027 in Germany is a unique fellowship opportunity for individuals dedicated to sustainability, innovation, and transformative change. With generous support, interdisciplinary collaboration, and a global research environment, this program empowers fellows to develop impactful solutions for today’s most pressing sustainability challenges. RIFS Fellow Program 2027 Details:
- Host Country: Germany
- Location: Potsdam
- Host Institute: Research Institute for Sustainability
- Affiliated Institution: GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences
- Duration: 6 – 12 Months (extendable up to 24 months)
- Eligibility: International Applicants
Benefits of the RIFS Fellow Program 2027:
- Monthly fellowship stipend
- Financial and organizational support for fellowship activities
- Opportunity to work within an international research community
- Networking opportunities with global experts and institutions
- Support for:
- Housing in Potsdam/Berlin
- Visa and residence procedures
- Health insurance
- School placements (if applicable)

Eligibility Criteria for RIFS Fellow Program 2027:
Applicants from all sectors and countries are encouraged to apply, including:
- Researchers and academics
- Public administration professionals
- Private sector experts
- Civil society representatives
- Media professionals
- Artists and creative practitioners
Additional requirements:
- The proposed project must align with the work of at least two RIFS research groups
- Applicants are strongly encouraged to contact RIFS researchers before applying
- All applications must be submitted in English
Required Documents:
Applicants must submit the following PDF documents:
- Project Proposal: Must use the official RIFS template
- Core document outlining the project and motivation
- CV and Achievements (Maximum 3 pages)
- Educational background
- Career highlights and achievements
- Optional Annex Publications
- Portfolio
- Additional supporting information
Deadline:
The application deadline to apply for the RIFS Fellow Program 2027 is 21 June 2026 (23:59 CET).

How to Apply for the RIFS Fellow Program 2027?
Follow these steps:
- Visit the official Research Institute for Sustainability website.
- Download the official project proposal template.
- Prepare all required documents in English.
- Complete the online application form.
- Upload all PDF documents.
- Submit your application before the deadline.
- Applications submitted via email will not be accepted.
