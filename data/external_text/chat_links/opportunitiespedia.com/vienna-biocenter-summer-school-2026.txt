Eligibility Criteria for the Vienna Biocenter Summer School 2026:
- Must study a Life Science-related degree, relevant to the program offered.
- Completed at least 2 years of university study by 30 June (e.g., 3rd year of a 4-year Bachelor, 1st year of a Master’s).
- 3 months of research experience is required.
- Strong academic record.
- High level of English proficiency.
- Proof of enrollment in the current and next academic year (if possible).
- MD applicants must have 3-4 years of study and relevant research experience, with plans to pursue a PhD in Life Sciences.
- Excludes degrees in veterinary medicine, medical engineering, agriculture, pharmacy, zoology, and non-lab-based Life Science programs.
