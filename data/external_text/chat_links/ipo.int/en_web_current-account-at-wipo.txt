Current Account at WIPO
Current Account at WIPO is a financial account, managed in Swiss Francs, designed to simplify the payment process for customers who regularly carry out transactions with WIPO. Current Account at WIPO is the most popular payment method in our application WIPO Pay.
You can use a Current Account at WIPO to pay WIPO online services such as Patents, Trademarks, Industrial Designs, Geographical Indications, Domain Name Disputes, Arbitration and Mediation, and WIPO Academy online courses.
Do you want to learn more?
Is Current Account at WIPO for me?
If you are a company or an individual having transactions on a regular basis with WIPO (at least several times per year), you can open a Current Account at WIPO.
When you hold a Current Account at WIPO, you:
- pay all WIPO services with your Current Account at WIPO
- replenish your Current Account at WIPO by lump sum payments
- personalize the management of your account

What would I like to do?
Open a Current Account at WIPO
Follow easy steps to open a Current Account at WIPO. Remember that you need a WIPO Account for this purpose.
Synchronize your Current Account and WIPO Account
To be able to pay with your Current Account at WIPO, you have to synchronize it first with your WIPO Account.
Access and manage your Current Account at WIPO
Learn about the roles you can assume in managing your Current Account at WIPO. Based on your role, your activities will differ.
Pay your fees with a Current Account at WIPO
Before making payments, ensure your Current Account at WIPO has sufficient funds. It is a good practice to set up a threshold notification: you can customize it to fit your needs.
Check balance and deposit funds in your Current Account at WIPO
- Consult all your past transactions and the current balance anytime, online on your Current Account at WIPO portal.
- Replenish your Current Account at WIPO by a bank transfer in Swiss Francs (CHF).
Close your Current Account at WIPO
You can close your Current Account at WIPO at anytime provided that your transactions are paid and completed. You will receive your remaining balance back to your bank account.
