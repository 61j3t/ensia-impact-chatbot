About the Program
The Algerian Startup Learning Expedition Program (ASEP) isn’t just a program—it’s a game-changer. Designed to supercharge Algerian startups, ASEP takes entrepreneurs beyond borders, right into the heart of the world’s most dynamic innovation ecosystems.
450 bold startups. 15 days of non-stop inspiration.
Get ready for exclusive access to trailblazing institutions, face-to-face sessions with industry leaders, and a front-row seat to cutting-edge technologies. It’s about making connections that matter, unlocking fresh ideas, and discovering the best practices that turn startups into global success stories.

FAQs
Below are some frequently asked questions about the
Algerian Startup Expedition Program.
Who is organising this program?
The program is an initiative of the Algerian government, overseen by the Ministry of Knowledge Economy, Startups, and Micro-enterprises, and executed by Algeria Venture.
Who can benefit from this program?
Labeled startups are eligible to participate in the program.
How many people can participate?
- A participating startup can send only one representative to the program.
- A participating startup can visit only one destination.
What expenses are covered for participants?
All expenses, including flight ticket (round-trip economy class), accommodation, meals and beverages, local transportation, and translation services.
Deadline to Register
The deadline to register is 15th of June 2025. Don’t miss this opportunity to be part of a transformative experience!
