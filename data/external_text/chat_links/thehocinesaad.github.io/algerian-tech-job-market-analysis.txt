68% of postings claim to want "full-stack" developers, but 82% of those heavily emphasize either frontend OR backend. True full-stack roles are rare — most companies want specialists wearing two hats.
"Junior" positions often require 2-3 years of experience. Entry-level opportunities represent only 18% of postings, yet 40% of job seekers are entry-level or career changers.
While 42% offer some remote flexibility, only 8% are fully remote. "Hybrid" often means 3-4 days in office, negating most remote work benefits for those outside Algiers.
The average job posting mentions 8-12 different technologies. This "kitchen sink" approach suggests companies don't know what they need, or want one person to do the work of three.
Only 12% mention AI/LLM tools, but this is rapidly increasing. Developers not learning AI integration now will find themselves uncompetitive within 12-18 months.
87% of opportunities are in Algiers. If you're in Oran, Constantine, or smaller cities, you're competing for just 13% of available positions, often requiring relocation.
