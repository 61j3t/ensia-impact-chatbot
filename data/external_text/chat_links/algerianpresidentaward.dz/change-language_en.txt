National recognition of scientific excellence
Valuing innovation and research
Established by presidential decree, this prestigious award honors outstanding scientific research that contributes to innovation and sustainable development in Algeria.
- Encourage excellence in scientific research
- Value innovation for national development
- Promote technology transfer
- Strengthen the link between research and socio-economic needs
- Support the knowledge economy
Fields of Excellence
The award recognizes research work in eight strategic fields for scientific and economic development.
Sciences and Technologies
Includes physics, chemistry, mathematics, computer science, engineering, technology, artificial intelligence, robotics, telecommunications, and related disciplines.
Human and Social Sciences
Includes philosophy, sociology, anthropology, history, geography, political science, economics, psychology, languages and literature, cultural studies, information and communication sciences, and related disciplines.
Health and Biomedical Sciences
Includes medicine, nursing, pharmacy, biomedical sciences, public health, life sciences, medical biotechnology, and related disciplines.
Environment and Sustainable Development
Includes environmental sciences, climatology, earth sciences, natural resource management, sustainable development, renewable energy, and related disciplines.
Food and Water
Includes food sciences, food technology, agriculture, soil science, water resource management, hydrology, and related disciplines.
Energy Security
Includes energy engineering, energy management, renewable energy sources, energy efficiency, and related disciplines.
Water Security
Includes water resource management, water technologies, water engineering, water policies, and related disciplines.
Peace and Security
Includes peace studies, security studies, international relations, conflicts, human rights, and related disciplines.
Eligibility criteria
-
Applicants must be Algerian nationals and reside in Algeria. Algerian researchers residing abroad may participate if their work has a direct impact on the national economy.
-
The nominated researcher is proposed by their higher education and scientific research institution.
-
Submitted works must fall within one of the listed fields.
-
A candidate may submit only one project, and a previous laureate cannot apply again before three years.
