Digital Transformation Review
Understanding technology is a very different skill set to running a business, so there’s a lot to gain from accessing expert advice.
We offer our clients a Digital Transformation Review to identify and outline the specific support they need to grow their business through digital transformation.
In a Digital Transformation Review your expert IT Director will assess your business’s current digital issues and identify opportunities for future growth.
It is tailor-made for each client focusing on their key issues and opportunities but often consists of:
- Internal Research – reviewing current technologies and operations relating to digital technology and data.
- Digital Workshop – discussing the needs of stakeholders as well as understanding the business and its customers to see what change is most relevant to facilitating growth. This may involve exploring Operational Agility, Customer Experience, or Digital Growth. Your Advisor will bring their experience and subject matter knowledge to support your understanding.
- Recommendations – providing a set of recommended options for change to improve IT and growth opportunities.
- Follow Up Meeting – discussing and, if needed, expanding on recommended changes and implementation strategies so you understand everything you need. As the client, you can decide on priorities and timescales, and decide if you want ongoing input from us.
