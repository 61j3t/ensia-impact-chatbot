The Scottish Region
Are you looking for part-time director level support for your business? Boardroom Advisors has a team of local advisors for businesses in the region of Scotland, including Edinburgh, Glasgow, Aberdeen, Lothian, and Grampian.
As well as the team of local advisors, Boardroom Advisors has a national team of over 150 Advisors. All of our Advisors have experience on Boards and have scaled up business before, so can provide their expertise for whatever challenges you scale-up or SME might face.
Our Regional Director for Boardroom Advisors, will help connect you with the right support for your business. From Board Advisory through to CEOs with specialisations from Strategy to Sales, there’s an Advisor to suit you. You can find a full overview of our services here.
Introduction to the Scottish Region
- UK Tech Innovation Index ranked Edinburgh as the 2nd top city in Top 10 UK cities for tech innovation after London.
- Edinburgh has recently been revealed by Tech Nation as the one of the UK’s leading tech hubs
- Edinburgh bagged the second overall ranking in the ‘mid-sized European city of the future’ category by the Financial Times’ fDi Intelligence European Cities of the Future 2020/21 report.
- In 2017 the GVA per head in the City of Edinburgh was £44,200, second to London which is £48,900. In the same year, the Finance and Insurance Sector GVA in Edinburgh was over £4 billion. It covered 19% of Edinburgh’s total GVA.
- Edinburgh came out as the Top City in Uswitch’s Best Place to Live in The UK Quality of Life Index, which assessed more than 130 statistical regions for different lifestyle factors.
-
Edinburgh named as UK’s most attractive city for inward investment according to the recent Arcadis Sustainable Cities Index, which assesses the top 100 cities in the world and how each city performs according to measures such as Economics, People and Planet.
Edinburgh, Scotland’s country capital, is home to an estimate of 520,000 residents, 78% of whom are working age and is considered as a higher percentage than any other major UK city.
Edinburgh’s bustling city centre, stunning cityscape and elegance and it’s historical and cultural attractions is a sight to behold which attracts million visitors every year.
Edinburgh is widely known for its visitor attraction — Edinburgh Castle, one of the oldest castles in the world and is considered alive with many exciting tales from its rich history as a royal residence, garrison, fortress and prison. It is also famous for the Edinburgh Festival Fringe — world’s largest arts festival which had around had around 2.8 million attendees in 2018.
In 2018, there were around 16.9 million visitor nights with a total spending of £1.7 billion in Edinburgh.
The core business sectors in Edinburgh include Banking & Finance, Technology & Electronics, Food & Drink, Life Sciences, Renewable Energy, Tourism, Education, Health, Distribution, Service, Real Estate and Information & Communication Services.
Edinburgh has been considered as one of the world’s most future-proof cities as identified by JLL’s City Momentum Index
Many business start-ups choose Edinburgh because it attracts the curious, innovative and a great pool of talent already living, working and studying in the city.
Edinburgh is accessible by all means of public transportation. Edinburgh airport serves 150 cities worldwide and handles nearly 15m. passengers p.a. It has a well- structured and well-planned transportation systems that makes living and doing business convenient and accessible both locally and globally.
