Key Distinctions Between a CMO Consultant vs Full-time CMO
When deciding between hiring a full-time Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) or a CMO consultant, there are important distinctions to consider in terms of cost, flexibility, and scope of engagement.
- Cost: A 2023 survey found that UK businesses saved an average of 30% on marketing leadership costs by hiring interim professionals. A full-time CMO requires a significant salary and benefits package, while a CMO consultant offers a flexible, cost-effective option where you only pay for the services you need.
- Flexibility: CMO consultants offer greater flexibility, stepping in to lead specific projects, provide short-term engagement, or offer ongoing support. A full-time CMO, however, is a permanent hire responsible for the long-term, day-to-day management of the marketing function.
- Expertise: CMO consultants bring diverse experience from working with multiple companies across industries, providing fresh perspectives and innovative solutions. Full-time CMOs may have deeper knowledge of your specific business but lack the broad expertise that a CMO consultant can offer.
- Short-Term or Project-Based Support: CMO consultants are typically brought in for short-term needs or project-based work, while full-time CMOs manage the entire marketing department on a long-term basis.
