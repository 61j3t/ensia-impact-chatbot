Key Distinctions Between a Commercial Director Consultant and a Full-Time Commercial Director
When deciding between hiring a commercial director consultant or a full-time commercial director, there are several key distinctions to consider in terms of cost, flexibility, and scope of engagement.
- Cost: A full-time commercial director requires a significant salary and benefits package of approximately £348,755 per year, while a consultant offers a more flexible, cost-effective solution for businesses needing short-term or part-time leadership.
- Flexibility: A commercial director consultant provides greater flexibility, stepping in to offer leadership on a part-time or project basis. A full-time commercial director is a permanent hire responsible for the long-term management of commercial functions.
- Expertise: Commercial director consultants often bring diverse experience from working across industries, offering fresh perspectives and innovative solutions. Full-time commercial directors may have more in-depth knowledge of your business but may lack the broad insights of a consultant.
- Short-Term or Project-Based Engagement: Commercial director consultants are typically hired for short-term needs or specific projects, while full-time commercial directors manage the ongoing commercial functions on a permanent basis.
