Download Scale Up Your Business: Build Your Board of Directors
What to consider when building your scale-up business’s Board of Directors.
This ebook looks at your Board of Directors as you scale up your business. It covers how your Board may need to change and grow, and what you may need to consider.
Topics covered include:
❖ What is the Board: The role of the Board of Directors in a business.
❖ The role of a director: Understanding the expectations and responsibilites of a member of the Board of Directors. Also covers the various postions a member may hold.
❖ What a Board needs: Learn the general needs of a Board such as what skills and experiences need to be covered and what minimums need to be met.
❖ Is your Board ready? What to think about when comparing your current business to the general needs of a scale-up business.
❖ Corporate governance: What rules and guidelines are often put in place to govern a Board of Directors.
