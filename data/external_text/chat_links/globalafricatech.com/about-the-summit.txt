About the Summit
Connectivity For Continental Power
Our Mission
The Global Africa Tech Summit 2026 is dedicated to advancing digital sovereignty, infrastructure development, and policy alignment across the African continent.
- Strategic
- Objectives
- Promote digital sovereignty and data governance
- Accelerate continental infrastructure development
- Foster policy harmonization across African nations
- Facilitate strategic investments in African tech
Our Vision
A continent where technological innovation, digital infrastructure, and strategic policy converge to create unprecedented economic growth and global influence. We envision an Africa that leads the world in connectivity, digital transformation, and technological sovereignty, where every nation is empowered through robust infrastructure and unified policy frameworks.
Infrastructure
Building the next-generation connectivity infrastructure that connects Africa to itself and the world.
Policy
Developing harmonized policy frameworks that enable digital sovereignty and continental integration.
Innovation
Accelerating African tech innovation and creating pathways for strategic investment and growth.
