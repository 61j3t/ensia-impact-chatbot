Airport
Houari Boumediene International Airport (ALG) is located approximately 45 kilometers from the International Conference Center of Algiers (CIC), the official venue of the Global Africa Tech Summit 2026. The transfer time between the airport and the venue is estimated at 40 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic conditions. A full range of transportation options, including official taxis and private transfer services, is available to ensure a smooth and comfortable journey for all international and national participants.
