Yes, Azure Document Intelligence—formerly part of Azure AI Services—is now rebranded as Document Intelligence in Foundry Tools. This change is part of a broader platform unification under Foundry, aligning these services with how developers build intelligent, agentic applications today.
Document Intelligence still offers the same powerful capabilities—like extracting text, tables, key-value pairs, and layout from documents, forms, and images using prebuilt and custom models. Document Intelligence is now a core tool within the Foundry ecosystem, designed to work seamlessly with other tools like Speech, Translator, and Content Understanding.
This rebrand helps clarify how Document Intelligence fits into the Foundry platform—making it easier to discover, orchestrate, and integrate into modern AI workflows for document automation, knowledge extraction, and agentic reasoning.
