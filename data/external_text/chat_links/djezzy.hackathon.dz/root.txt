About the Hackathon
Djezzy is launching its pioneering event in technological innovation in Algeria through a hackathon that brings together top students to compete in creating promising projects. This event reflects Djezzy’s commitment to supporting innovation, particularly within the academic community, by providing student entrepreneurs with the opportunity to develop innovative solutions that contribute to advancing digital transformation and building the future.

Program Agenda
Three transformative days of creativity, collaboration, and innovation. From the first idea to the final pitch, teams will experience what it takes to bring a startup to life.
The first day marks the start of the hackathon journey. Participants register, connect, and form teams before diving into brainstorming sessions. With mentor support, they begin turning their ideas into clear concepts, setting the foundation for the prototyping phase.
Teams dedicate this day to transforming their ideas into tangible prototypes and refining their business models. Workshops on pitching, lean canvas, and financial modeling help strengthen their projects, while mentors provide personalized guidance as teams prepare for the final presentation.
Final prototypes are completed and rehearsed before being presented to the jury. Each team delivers their pitch, followed by jury deliberation. The event closes with the announcement of winners, awards ceremony, and networking.
