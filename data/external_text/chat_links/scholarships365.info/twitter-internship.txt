Eligibility Criteria for Twitter Internship Program
In order to qualify for a place on the Twitter Virtual Internship Program you will need to meet the following requirements:
The eligibility criteria for Twitter Internship Program vary in the applicant’s region. To apply for a Twitter internship, candidates must fulfill the following requirements
JAPAC (Japan, Asia, and the Pacific):
- Interns should be of 18+ age and must be currently enrolled in a two-four year academic institution
- For the selected internees it is compulsory to complete the full internship duration.
EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa):
- Applicants must be of 18+ age at the time of application and should be currently enrolled in a three - four-year academic institution working on an undergrad, masters, MBA, or Ph.D. degree.
- Interns should also be able to complete the full virtual 12-week internship program
North and Latin America:
- Applicants must be of at least 18+ age and maybe currently enrolled in a two-four year academic institution
- Applicants must be willing for full-time enrollment in the current and final year of school.
- They must have to complete a full 12-week virtual internship duration.
