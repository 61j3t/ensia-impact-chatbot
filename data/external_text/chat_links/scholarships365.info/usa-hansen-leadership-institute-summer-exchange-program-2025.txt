What are the Eligibility Requirements for the Hansen Leadership Institute Summer exchange program in US 2025?
For International Students:
- Must have an upper-division undergraduate completed 2 years of college.
- Graduate students whose emphasis is on international relations, diplomacy, international economic development, or other related areas
- Applicant should be 20 to 25 years old by the time he/she will begin the program.
- Must demonstrate proven leadership in his or her community through service and initiatives nationally or regionally
- English language proficiency is required for an applicant; his academic advisor should verify.
- An international passport with a U.S. visa should be in place by the time of the start of the program.
- Preference will be given to students who have never visited the United States.
For U.S. Students:
- The applicant should have completed 2 years of college as an upper-division undergraduate.
- Applicants who are graduate students, specializing in international relations, diplomacy, international economic development, or related areas.
- The applicant must be aged between 20 and 25 years at the commencement of the program.
- The applicant should have demonstrated leadership in community service, public service, or through involvement with a non-governmental organization.
