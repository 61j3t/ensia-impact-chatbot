Jan. 2025
During this phase, you will gain access to the official Company Program platform, which includes the full suite of online courses.
Additionally, this platform will connect you with a coach and a team, ensuring you are grouped with others if you registered as part of a group.
April 2025
During this phase, you will start your in-person training with your coach(es) while simultaneously working to create your company's business plan, financial plans, and prototype.
May 2025
During this phase, you will submit a file containing your company's plans and presentations.
10 finalists will be chosen from this phase.
June 2025
The 10 finalists will compete in the annual young entrepreneurs' celebration of INJAZ El Djazair, where only one company will be crowned "The Best Company of The Year 2025"
Nov. 2025
One team from the local Young Entrepreneurs Celebration will be chosen to compete against 13 other winners from member nations all over the MENA region.
