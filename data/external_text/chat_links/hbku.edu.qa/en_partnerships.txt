Overview
At Hamad Bin Khalifa University (HBKU), Strategic partnerships are the foundation of transformative impact and progress. Those partnerships are designed to strengthen interdisciplinary academia, advance research and innovation, empower communities, and facilitate connections between the Arab world and global ecosystems. Over the past decade, these collaborations have been central to HBKU’s position as a hub for knowledge creation and practical real-world solutions.
As a forward-thinking university, HBKU collaborates closely with leading universities, industry leaders, and governmental entities to address global challenges while contributing to national development objectives. Our partnerships span multiple disciplines, thereby enhancing interdisciplinary learning, advancing cutting-edge research, and creating entrepreneurship opportunities that align with Qatar’s National Vision 2030. Through these collaborations, we effectively bridge the gap between academia and societal and economic needs, ensuring that our programs remain aligned with the evolving needs of the workforce and industry. By harnessing the expertise of renowned academics and industrial partners, HBKU continues to shape the next generation of leaders, innovators, and transformative change-makers.
