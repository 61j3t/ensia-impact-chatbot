Frequently Asked Questions
Have a question? Find your answer below and save time! View All FAQs
Can I try IxDF before committing?
Yes.
When you join IxDF, you get full access to everything right from day one.
Plus, you're covered by our 14-day money-back guarantee.
This gives you two full weeks to try IxDF, jump into courses, and see how we can support your career growth.
If it's not the right fit, simply cancel within 14 days, and we'll issue a full refund.
How flexible are IxDF courses? Can I learn anytime, anywhere?
All IxDF courses are self-paced and 100% online.
You can learn whenever and wherever it suits you, whether that's over morning coffee, during a lunch break, or at midnight in your pajamas.
There are no required live sessions, so you never have to worry about missing a class. Once you enroll, you control the pace. You can finish a course course in a few weeks or take your time over several months.
Your course materials, conversations, and past work will always be available, so you can revisit them anytime.
While you can learn solo at your own pace, you won't be alone. IxDF courses include group exercises, active discussion forums, and a worldwide network of peers to exchange insights with.
Plus, you have the option to join (or host!) Local Meetups in over 450 cities to connect with fellow learners in person.
IxDF courses fit into your life. Not the other way around.
Will I receive IxDF Course Certificates?
Each time you complete a course, master class, or career program, you earn an industry-recognized certificate that proves you've completed an evidence-based curriculum taught by industry leaders.
You can add it to your LinkedIn profile, resumé, or job applications.
You can see an example of a Course Certificate at the bottom of the Course Catalogue.
There’s no specified time by which certificates must be awarded. Instead, they’re given to you as and when you’ve answered all quiz questions. This applies even if the questions are answered long after the official end date of the course.
My company wants to pay for my membership. Should I choose a Company Membership?
If your company wants to cover your membership, just use your company's card at checkout. You'll get a downloadable invoice for your records.
If your colleagues want to join too, your manager might want to explore our Company Memberships, which are designed for teams.
Will I be learning alone?
That's up to you!
With IxDF, you can learn entirely on your own at your own pace, or you can tap into a global community for support, feedback, and networking.
Prefer solo learning? No problem. Work through courses independently, distraction-free, on your own schedule.
Want input from others? You'll have plenty of ways to network, connect, and collaborate.
Get feedback and insights: Ask questions, share ideas, and get valuable input in our course discussion forums.
Join Local Group Meetups: Connect with UX design professionals in your area at IxDF events held in 450+ cities worldwide.
Find your people: Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned pro, you'll have peers to exchange ideas with, solve problems, and gain fresh perspectives.
Whether you thrive in quiet study or love bouncing ideas off others, IxDF gives you the flexibility to learn your way.
Are all the courses really free for members?
Yes.
You get unlimited access to all IxDF courses at no extra cost. Take as many courses as you want without paying extra. You can literally save thousands of dollars.
Are IxDF courses suitable for beginners or advanced learners?
Our courses, master classes, and career programs cover everything from the fundamentals to advanced topics.
Whether you're completely new to design or an experienced professional, you'll find courses, master classes and career programs at your level.
Can I use IxDF to switch careers without starting over?
Yes.
Your past experience is an advantage, not a roadblock.
Many of our members have successfully transitioned into UX design, product design, and other design-related roles.
Our courses help you build on the skills you already have, whether you come from marketing, development, psychology, business, or another field. UX design thrives on problem-solving, communication, and critical thinking, so chances are, you're already bringing valuable skills to the table.
Read How to Successfully Switch to a UX Career in 2026 to see how other members have done it.
Can I really learn UX design if I'm not “techy”?
Yes.
UX design isn't about coding or being a tech wizard. It's about understanding people and creating great experiences.
If you can think critically, solve problems, and empathize with people, you already have the foundation for UX design. IxDF courses break down concepts into clear, practical steps, so you can easily pick things up without any technical background.
Many successful UX designers come from fields like marketing, psychology, business, customer service, and even healthcare. So if you're curious and willing to learn, UX design is absolutely within your reach!
