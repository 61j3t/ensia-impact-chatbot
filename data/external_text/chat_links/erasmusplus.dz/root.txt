WHO WE ARE?
The mandate of the National Erasmus+ Office in Algeria (NEO-Algeria) covers all support, promotion and dissemination activities related to the higher and vocational education dimension of the Erasmus+ Programme 2021-2027. NEO-Algeria contributes to the following overall objectives: Improve the awareness, visibility, relevance, effectiveness and impact of the international components of the Erasmus+ programme. Contribute to its promotion and dissemination in Algeria.
