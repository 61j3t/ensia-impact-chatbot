[Title: Follow-up Meeting on University 4.0 TAM - National Erasmus+ Office Algeria]
[Backend: httpx]
[Fetched: 2026-05-15T11:16:48+00:00]

An online follow-up meeting on the University 4.0 technical assistance mission, recently held at Ferhat Abbas University – Sétif 1, took place on 19 November between the Erasmus+ Higher Education Reform Experts (HEREs) and the National Commission for University 4.0, with the participation of EU experts Dr. Elisa Negri and Prof. Pierluigi Leone.
The meeting aimed to discuss the final version of the recommendations resulting from the event, in view of supporting the implementation of 4th-generation universities in Algeria.
