[Title: Erasmus+ Ranking of Algerian Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) - National Erasmus+ Office Algeria]

We are delighted to share with you the ranking of Algerian Higher Education Institutions participating in the Erasmus+ (ICM) exchange projects for the 2022 and 2023 proposal calls. The ICM (International Credit Mobility) exchange programme facilitate international exchanges for students, academic staff, and administrative personnel from Algerian higher education institutions with their European counterparts.
We extend our sincere appreciation to all participants for their invaluable contributions to the continued success of the Erasmus+ program in Algeria. We also urge all higher education institutions to actively engage in the ongoing call. Your involvement will further strengthen Algeria’s standing on the global stage of higher education.
