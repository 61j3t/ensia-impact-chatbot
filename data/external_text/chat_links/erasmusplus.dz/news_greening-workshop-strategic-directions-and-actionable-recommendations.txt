The “Higher Education and Greening in Algeria and Lebanon” workshop, held on November 20–21, 2024, aimed to explore the pivotal role of Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) in advancing sustainability through greening initiatives. This document presents a comprehensive overview of the workshop, emphasizing the strategic directions and actionable recommendations developed through expert presentations, interactive discussions, and collaborative reflections. Participants delved into the challenges and opportunities for HEIs in fostering environmentally sustainable practices, focusing on policies, curricula, and infrastructure adaptations. The workshop’s outcomes provide valuable insights for shaping future efforts in integrating sustainability into higher education systems in both Algeria and Lebanon.
