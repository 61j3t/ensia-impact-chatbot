Bridging the Innovation Gap
In Algeria, innovation is on the move: talents are emerging, initiatives are multiplying. Yet, the ecosystem remains fragmented, difficult to read, and weakly connected to the international stage. What's missing is a catalyst — a connector that can structure and empower the ecosystem.
EYCONET is that catalyst. Born from a strong conviction that:
"To succeed, to be competitive and sustainable, innovative projects need a structured, dynamic, and interconnected environment."
EYCONET bridges startups, experts, investors, and institutions in Algeria and beyond. Our digital platform provides mapping, performance indicators (KPIs), mentoring, and direct access to markets and funding. We bring visibility, structure, and credibility to every actor in the ecosystem.
Empowering Through Action
EYCONET acts as a catalyst for the entrepreneurial and innovation ecosystem by:
Delivering a powerful digital platform for connection and collaboration
Mobilizing a network of engaged experts and mentors
Providing measurable impact through clear performance indicators (KPIs)
Creating bridges to markets, financing opportunities, and international networks
Every action we take is designed to generate tangible results: growth, visibility, skills development, and access to investment.

We aim to help position Algeria as a recognized hub of innovation and startups, locally anchored yet globally connected.
