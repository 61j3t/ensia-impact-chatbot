Privacy Policy
Effective Date: April 27, 2025
Notiverse (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (“App”).
1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
-
Microphone Access:
We use your microphone to allow you to record voice notes. Voice recordings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers unless explicitly stated. -
Camera Access:
We use your device’s camera to allow you to take pictures for image-based notes. Images are stored locally on your device and are not shared unless you choose to back them up manually. -
Device and Usage Data (via Firebase Analytics):
We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (such as session duration, features used, and app performance). This helps us improve the app experience. -
Crash Reports (via Firebase Crashlytics):
In the event of a crash or technical issue, we collect crash data through Google Firebase Crashlytics. This information includes technical device details and crash logs, but no personally identifiable information (PII).
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
- Provide core features like voice note-taking and photo note storage.
- Improve app functionality, user experience, and stability.
- Monitor app performance, diagnose technical issues, and fix bugs.
3. Data Storage and Security
- Notes (text, voice and images) are stored locally on your device by default.
- Analytics and crash reports are securely transmitted and processed by Google Firebase services.
- We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.
4. Sharing of Information
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
However, some data (such as anonymized analytics and crash reports) may be processed by trusted third-party service providers, specifically:
- Google Firebase Analytics (analytics)
- Google Firebase Crashlytics (error reporting)
These services comply with applicable data protection regulations.
5. Permissions Required
- Microphone: To record voice notes.
- Camera: To capture images for notes.
- Storage: To save recordings and images on your device.
- Internet Access: To send anonymous analytics and crash reports (optional and non-personal).
6. Your Privacy Rights
You can manage your app permissions at any time through your device settings.
You may also delete any locally stored notes manually through the app or your device storage.
To opt-out of Firebase Analytics data collection, you can disable analytics tracking in your device settings if supported.
7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app or on our website.
We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
