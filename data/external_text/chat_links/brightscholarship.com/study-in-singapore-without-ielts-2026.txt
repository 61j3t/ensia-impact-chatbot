How to Apply for Study in Singapore Without IELTS?
There are multiple ways to Study without IELTS Test in Singapore. Details is given below:
- English Speaking Countries: If you’re from an English-speaking country such as the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand, Then you don’t need of IELTS test.
- Previous Degree Instruction Language in English: Have completed your bachelor’s or master’s degrees with English as the primary instruction language exempts you from the IELTS requirement.
- English Proficiency Report: Submit a letter of English Proficiency if your prior degree was conducted in English at the same university.
- Apply to IELTS Waiver Programs: In Singapore, there are programs that waive the requirement for the IELTS exam. These programs are designed to accommodate international students who may not have taken the IELTS test or who do not meet the traditional English language proficiency requirement.
