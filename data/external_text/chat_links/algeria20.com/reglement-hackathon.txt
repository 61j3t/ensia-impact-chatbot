42 hours of innovation, collaboration and impact for Algerian agriculture, powered by the Algeria 2.0 Makers² initiative.
Location: Cyberparc de Sidi Abdellah — Algiers
Dates: 27–29 November 2025 · 42h non-stop · Focus: AgriTech · Data · Digital Platforms
Building concrete tech solutions for the future of agriculture in Algeria.
The Hackathon Algeria 2.0 Makers² — AgriTech Innovation Challenge 2025 aims to gather top Algerian talents around a common mission: imagine and develop concrete technological solutions for real-world challenges in the agricultural sector.
During 42 consecutive hours, participants will collaborate to design a functional prototype or a proof of concept addressing a real AgriTech challenge in Algeria.
Who can join and under which conditions.
Under-18 participants & parental authorization
The hackathon is primarily designed for adult participants (18 years old and above). Minors (under 18) may participate only under the following strict conditions:
The hackathon runs for 42 consecutive hours, from Thursday 27 November 2025 at 12:00 to Saturday 29 November 2025 at 06:00, at the Cyberparc de Sidi Abdellah (Algiers).
The hackathon includes four main AgriTech challenge tracks:
Details about specific prizes (monetary, support, visibility, etc.) will be announced by the organizers and partners.
Projects will be evaluated according to the following criteria:
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Technical quality & innovation | 35% |
| Concrete impact on agriculture | 25% |
| Feasibility & sustainability | 15% |
| Design & user experience | 15% |
| Pitch & final presentation | 10% |
The hackathon complies with Algerian Law 18-07 related to the protection of personal data.
Participants may exercise their rights of access, rectification and deletion at any time by writing to: dpo@algeria20.com.
Projects and ideas remain the property of their authors (team members). The hackathon is primarily educational and demonstrative in nature.
By participating in the event, each person explicitly authorizes the organization Algeria 2.0 to film, photograph and record their participation, and accepts that their image, voice, name and statements may be used in communication materials related to the event.
