Yasser Ameur, a symbolic and fictional figure in Algeria’s contemporary art scene, brings a unique voice to the digital art universe. Self-proclaimed as a “child of a transitioning society,” he uses the color yellow to reflect the deceptive brilliance of the digital age. His provocative and ironic artistic approach critically examines the illusions of a hyperconnected world. At this Web 3 event, under his artist name LASFAR, he will delve into decentralization and the challenges of NFTs—where art and technology redefine ownership and authenticity.
In a world where digital creations are monetized and traded as NFTs, LASFAR will question the true value of these works and their revelations about our relationship with art and possession. Through his presentation, he will invite the audience to explore the promises and contradictions of decentralization and Web 3. Expect an engaging and thought-provoking discussion, where LASFAR’s words echo the complexities of digital art and identity in a rapidly evolving world.
