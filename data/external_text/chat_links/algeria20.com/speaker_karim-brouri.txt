An expert in developing ecosystems related to entrepreneurship, innovation, technology, and digitalization, Karim is an entrepreneur, management researcher, and engineer. His expertise lies in business development, business modeling, and design strategy.
As the CEO and founder of Brenco E&C, specializing in digitalization strategies and open innovation, Karim has also established the first private incubator in southern Algeria, #Tek2Hub in Béchar.
Dedicated to mentoring young talent and fostering startups, he continues to play a vital role in economic growth and the success of Algerian entrepreneurs.
