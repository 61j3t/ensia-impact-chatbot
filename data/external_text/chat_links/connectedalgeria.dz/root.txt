Connected Algeria 2025 is a landmark event dedicated to driving the nation’s digital transformation. VISION
2029
Algeria is on a path to becoming a regional leader in digital transformation by 2029. With cutting-edge infrastructure, a thriving innovation ecosystem, and a commitment to digital inclusion, the country is laying the foundation for a future driven by technology. Algeria 2025 is a key milestone in this journey, shaping policies, fostering partnerships, and accelerating progress.

"Algeria is committed to digital transformation as a driver of economic growth and innovation.

Connected Algeria 2025 is a major step forward in realizing this vision, fostering collaboration between institutions, businesses, and innovators to build a stronger, more connected future."
