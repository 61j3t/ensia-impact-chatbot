About Connected Algeria 2025
Connected Algeria?
Connected Algeria is the premier event dedicated to shaping Algeria’s digital future.
More than just a conference, it is a catalyst for innovation and collaboration, bringing together key players from across the tech ecosystem—startups, policymakers, investors, and global industry leaders. Through cutting-edge discussions, breakthrough showcases, and strategic networking, Connected Algeria accelerates the nation's transition towards a competitive, digitally-driven economy.
VISION 2029
By 2029, Algeria aims to become a regional leader in digital transformation, fostering an ecosystem where technology drives economic growth, innovation flourishes, and global partnerships thrive. Positioned at the crossroads of Africa, Europe, Asia, and America, Algeria has the potential to be a key player in the global digital landscape. With world-class fiber-optic connectivity, a dynamic talent pool, and a thriving tech ecosystem, the country is on the path to becoming a hub for innovation and investment. Connected Algeria 2025 serves as a vital milestone in this journey, bringing together stakeholders to define the roadmap towards a connected, smart, and resilient future.
Strategic Goals
Position Algeria as a leading digital hub in the region
Accelerate the adoption of emerging technologies
Foster collaboration between the public and private sectors
Support the growth of startups and SMEs
Attract foreign investment in Algeria’s ICT sector
Enhance digital skills and workforce readiness
Key Themes
Digital Transformation & E-Government
Accelerating the digitalization of public institutions to enhance efficiency, accessibility, and transparency. Establishing a regulatory framework to support digital expansion, simplifying administrative processes, and ensuring equitable access to public digital services.
Telecom & Technological Infrastructure
Expanding fiber-optic networks, accelerating 5G deployment, and preparing for future technologies such as 6G. Strengthening national data centers and investing in high-performance computing to support digital sovereignty.
Technology Parks & Innovation Hubs
Boosting dedicated innovation zones to attract startups, tech SMEs, and international investors. Enhancing existing cyberparks like Sidi Abdellah and integrating them into global innovation networks while creating new technology zones across Algeria’s regions.
Advanced Technologies & Industrial Transformation
Encouraging the adoption of AI, IoT, and blockchain across key economic sectors. Promoting automation and smart technologies in industries such as energy, agriculture, healthcare, and manufacturing to drive productivity and economic growth.
Cybersecurity & Data Protection
Safeguarding critical infrastructure, personal data, and national digital assets. Implementing advanced cybersecurity measures, enforcing data protection laws, and strengthening Algeria’s cyber resilience through education and awareness.
