See Our Sponsors & Partners
IIE provides a wide range of services and manages or administers programs for the organizations listed below. In addition to those sponsors and funders named here, IIE is deeply grateful to the 140 foreign governments and the hundreds of colleges and universities across the United States that participate in and contribute to the success of the Fulbright Student Programs, the Hubert Humphrey Fellows Program, and the Fulbright Scholar Programs, along with the sponsoring agency, the U.S. Department of State. We would also like to thank the numerous other institutions and donors that provided various forms of support to other programs administered by IIE. Institutional sponsors include:
