Study Tours and Delegations
IIE regularly leads international delegations and study tours for higher education representatives, stakeholder groups, and government officials from the United States and around the world. IIE is committed to creating customized experiences for important face-to-face interactions and hands-on cross-cultural experiences.
Build International Partnerships
IIE creates cross-border connections between higher education institutions, faculty, and students to cultivate research collaboration, foster diplomacy, build capacity, and exchange ideas.
Expand Study Abroad Opportunities
Access IIE’s resources for institutions and organizations to reach your goals to increase and diversify U.S. students studying abroad. Mobilizing young people to develop personal, academic, and professional competencies from an international experience is critical in today’s interconnected world.
Promote IIE-managed Scholarships for Study Abroad
IIE implements many of the world’s most prestigious and effective scholarship programs sponsored by governments, corporations and foundations that advance access to study abroad.
