[Title: شراكة بين الجزائر والصين لتدشين مختبر مشترك للذكاء الاصطناعي]
[Language: ar]
[Modified: 2025-03-12T15:59:24]

We are pleased to announce that the National School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA) in Algeria and the Artificial Intelligence Research Institute of Tsinghua University (AIR) in China have signed a memorandum of understanding to establish the Algerian-Chinese Joint Laboratory for Artificial Intelligence.

This strategic partnership follows the memorandum of understanding on the joint establishment of the Technology Transfer Center between China and Algeria, which was signed on July 18, 2023, by the Algerian Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research and the Chinese Ministry of Science and Technology.
