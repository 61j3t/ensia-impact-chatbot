Course Description:

In this module, students will be introduced to a selected number of advanced fields of research and industrial applications where artificial intelligence is being used. This includes topics from other disciplines including agriculture, medicine, biological sciences and economics. From the applicative side, various industrial case studies will be discussed where AI plays a pivotal role.
