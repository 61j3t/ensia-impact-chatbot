Course Description:
This course is a continuation of Continuous Mathematics 1 and covers topics in multivariate calculus, numerical series, and Fourier series. The course begins with a study of multivariate numerical and vector functions, including partial derivatives, gradients, and optimization. This leads into a study of multiple integrals. The course then moves on to numerical series, and the study of sequences and series of functions. Finally, the course covers Fourier series, including convergence and periodic functions.
Prerequisite :
Continuous Mathematics 1
Evaluation Method :
Coursework (40 %) + Final Exam (60%)
Course Content
Multivariate Numerical and Vector Functions
Multiple Integrals
Numerical Series
Sequence and series of functions
Integer Series
Fourier Series
