            Vectors to Values ENSIA Incubator (V2V)

          The
Vectors to Values ENSIA Incubator (V2V)
 empowers ENSIA students to transform innovative ideas into real-world solutions in the field of Artificial Intelligence. It bridges the gap between academic knowledge and practical application.

            Core Concepts

Vectors:
 Represent the fundamental elements of AI, forming the backbone of information processing and inspiring students to innovate.

Values:
 Reflect the tangible impact and benefits generated by applying these ideas across technological, entrepreneurial, and social domains.

            Practical Innovation at ENSIA

            V2V offers an environment where students tackle real-world challenges, analyze authentic data, and develop entrepreneurial mindsets. Projects are designed to create concrete, value-driven solutions that bridge theory and practice.

          Actions Speak Louder than Words!

The V2V Team

        The V2V incubator brings together a diverse team of professionals and researchers who combine technical expertise with strong leadership and human skills. They are specialists in their fields and are highly motivated to support innovation and add value.

A multidisciplinary group including AI specialists, researchers, entrepreneurs, and field practitioners.

Team members equipped with essential soft skills such as communication, collaboration, adaptability, and problem-solving.

Leaders driven by motivation, commitment, and a strong desire to help students and startups grow.

Individuals who combine scientific knowledge with empathy, creativity, and a willingness to create real impact.
