The National School of Artificial Intelligence offers 5-years Engineering Degrees which is the equivalent for studying towards an undergraduate Bachelor degree followed with a Master Degree. Students are taught various concepts and principles related to artificial intelligence. In terms of higher education, ENSIA has the following fundamental mission objectives in the field of Artificial Intelligence

Ensure the teaching and training of highly qualified engineers for the benefit of the socio-economic sectors

Introduce students to the methods of scientific research and provide training through and for research

Contribute to the production and dissemination of knowledge, its acquisition, and development

Promote lifelong education, development, and retraining activities for executives in socio-economic sectors

Instill the concepts of innovation, transfer of technology, and entrepreneurship into the teaching programs

Generalize the digital culture.
