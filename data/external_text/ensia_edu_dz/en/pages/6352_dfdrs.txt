Directeur Adjoint – ENSIA

The
Directeur Adjoint Chargé de la Formation Doctorale et de la Recherche Scientifique, du Développement Technologique, de l’Innovation et de la Promotion de l’Entrepreneuriat
 (Deputy Director in charge of Doctoral Training and Scientific Research, Technological Development, Innovation, and the Promotion of Entrepreneurship) plays a critical role in fostering advanced research, innovation, and entrepreneurial spirit within our institution. This position is responsible for overseeing the highest levels of academic training, promoting cutting-edge research, and translating that research into tangible benefits for society.

Key Responsibilities

Doctoral Training Management

Organizing and overseeing all aspects of doctoral training programs

Ensuring that doctoral training adheres to all applicable regulations

Ensuring the smooth operation of the doctoral programs

Research Leadership & Promotion

Promoting and invigorating the research agenda of the school

Monitoring the research activities conducted in labs and research units

Leading efforts to valorize research outcomes

Following the improvement of teacher-researchers

Coordinating work of all scientific committees

Collaboration and Development

Facilitating collaboration with other institutions

Encouraging networking and exchanges with national and international organizations

Fostering Excellence in Research

Supporting and recognizing scientific output

Developing strategies to promote research excellence

Organizing scientific events, seminars and workshops

Keeping up-to-date with laboratory activities

Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Promoting and supporting innovation and entrepreneurship

Ensuring all innovative productions are protected

Keeping abreast of new technology developments

Assisted By

Chef de Service de la Formation de Troisième Cycle et de la Recherche Scientifique

Focuses on the oversight of doctoral programs and research initiatives.

Chef de Service du Suivi des Activités de Recherche et de la Valorisation de ses Résultats

Monitors and helps improve research outputs.

Chef de Service de l’Innovation et de la Promotion de l’Entrepreneuriat

Leads initiatives to promote entrepreneurship and the commercialization of research findings.

Chef de Service du Développement Technologique

Focuses on activities related to new technology development and applications.

Our Commitment

The
Directeur Adjoint Chargé de la Formation Doctorale et de la Recherche Scientifique, du Développement Technologique, de l’Innovation et de la Promotion de l’Entrepreneuriat
 is dedicated to cultivating a dynamic research environment that advances knowledge, fosters innovation, and produces graduates ready to contribute meaningfully to society. We aim to position the school as a leader in doctoral training, research, and entrepreneurship.
