Directeur Adjoint – ENSIA

The
Directeur Adjoint Chargé des Systèmes d’Information et de Communication et des Relations Extérieures
 (Deputy Director in charge of Information and Communication Systems and External Relations) is responsible for managing the school’s internal and external communication, developing our digital infrastructure, and promoting our institution’s image and partnerships with external stakeholders. This role is crucial in maintaining transparency, facilitating efficient communication, and strengthening the school’s reputation within the community and beyond.

Key Responsibilities

Internal & External Communications

Creating and managing various communication materials, such as the school bulletin and website

Ensuring effective, clear communication across the institution

Following the best practices for communications through social networks

Digital Infrastructure & Development

Overseeing the development and maintenance of the school’s IT infrastructure and network

Guaranteeing the integration and management of the basic IT systems

Promoting the use of digital technology within our school

Information Management

Developing and implementing procedures for information collection and processing

Ensuring all necessary information reaches the concerned bodies and/or persons

Public Relations

Developing and managing the school’s relations with its surrounding environment

Proposing and managing activities and programs with external partners

Ensuring smooth running for the organization of all school events

Data & Statistics

Maintaining and updating the school’s statistical data

Monitoring all students and alumni

Partnerships and Collaboration

Developing and maintaining relations with other educational institutions

Leading initiatives with the public and private sector

Assisted By

Chef de Service des Systèmes d’Information et des Réseaux

Focuses on the management of the IT infrastructure, network security, and all technical aspects related to our systems.

Chef de Service de la Veille, des Statistiques et de la Prospective

Responsible for gathering and analyzing all types of data related to our activities as well as all planning operations and future strategies.

Chef de Service de la Communication et des Relations Extérieures

Manages the external communication efforts, PR and marketing.

Our Commitment

The
Directeur Adjoint Chargé des Systèmes d’Information et de Communication et des Relations Extérieures
 is dedicated to ensuring seamless communication, robust digital infrastructure, and strong relationships with all stakeholders. We strive to enhance the school’s reputation and facilitate productive exchanges through innovation and effective communication practices.
