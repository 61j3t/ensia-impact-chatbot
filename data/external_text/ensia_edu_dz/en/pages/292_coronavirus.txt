Covid-19 Guidelines

Social distancing (2 meters) must be maintained at all times.
Lecture theatres, offices, classrooms, and other common areas are operating at reduced capacity to maintain social distancing.
Videoconferencing software is available to students and staff to encourage and facilitate online (rather than face-to-face) meetings and gatherings.
The use of others’ phones, stationery, and personal items must be avoided as much as possible to reduce the spread of the virus.
Cleaning and disinfecting of common areas are carried out in a regular basis in order to ensure the health and safety of students, staff, and visitors.
All staff, students, and visitors must wear a mask at all times and wash/sanitize their hands frequently.
Stay at home and avoid contact with other people if you have tested positive for Covid-19 or if you have any symptoms of it. You have to call or email the administration of the school as early as you can to inform them.
