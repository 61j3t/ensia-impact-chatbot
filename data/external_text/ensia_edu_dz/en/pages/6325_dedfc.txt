Directeur Adjoint – ENSIA

The
Deputy Director in charge of Teaching, Diplomas, and Continuing Education
 plays a vital role in the academic administration of our institution. This position is responsible for overseeing the quality and compliance of our teaching programs, managing the conferral of diplomas, and developing continuing education initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

Supervising the Teaching Process

Monitoring and evaluating the effectiveness of all teaching activities

Ensuring the smooth progress of the courses

Guaranteeing that training programs align with the departments’ development plan

Diploma Issuance

Making sure that all procedures for issuing degrees comply with regulations

Continuing Education and Training

Developing and promoting training and professional development opportunities

Overseeing and evaluating training sessions

Class Preparatory Oversight

Overseeing the smooth operation of the Preparatory Class training program

Quality Control & Compliance

Ensuring compliance with current rules about enrollment and academic progress

Coordinating with pedagogical committees for learning plans alignment

Student Management

Maintaining and updating the student registry

Gathering educational materials and exam questions by subject

Enhancing Academic Standards

Ensuring quality standards in teaching and learning

Encouraging on-going pedagogical evaluation and adjustment

Monitoring student attendance and class progression

Assisted By

Chef de Service des Enseignements, de l’Évaluation et des Diplômes

Oversees the core teaching programs, assessment protocols and diploma issuance.

Chef de Service de la Formation Continue et des Stages

Manages the planning and delivery of continuous learning programs and placement of internships.

Chef de Service du Suivi des Contenus et de la Production Pédagogiques et de l’Assurance Qualité

Focuses on the quality and alignment of the educational materials and methodologies.

Our Commitment

The
Directeur Adjoint Chargé des Enseignements, des Diplômes, et de la Formation Continue
, is dedicated to creating a robust educational experience that prepares our students for future success while ensuring a continuous evolution of our academic programs. This is supported by promoting best practices in teaching methodologies, compliance in certification, and opportunities for lifelong learning.
