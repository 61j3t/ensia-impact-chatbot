1
Lessons are organized in 2 preparatory years specific to the school, followed by 3 years of specialization.

2
At the end of the 2 preparatory years, a percentage of the best students ranked according to their averages will be automatically admitted to the 3rd year.

3
The rest of the students will take a competition that will be open to other students from major computer science schools; the best students in the competition will complete the list of students admitted to the 3rd year of ENSIA. Company immersion internships are organized during semesters 4, 6, and 8 (i.e. 2nd, 3rd, and 4th years).
