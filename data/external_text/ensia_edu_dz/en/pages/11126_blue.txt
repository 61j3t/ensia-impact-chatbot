BLUE | Bureau de Liaison Université-Entreprise – ENSIA

        The Bridge Between
ENSIA
 & Industry

        The BLUE Office strengthens cooperation between ENSIA and the socio‑economic sector by promoting internships, applied research projects, partnerships and student employability.

Internships

          Facilitation of internships and placements, especially for final‑year projects (PFE).

Projects

          Support for joint academic–industrial projects and collaborative initiatives.

Partnerships

          Structured collaboration between ENSIA, companies and institutions through long‑term projects.

Strategic Objectives & Missions

        BLUE serves both ENSIA and its partners: opening the university to the socio‑economic world while offering companies a clear access point to talent and research.

For ENSIA & Students

              Promote openness to the socio‑economic ecosystem and identify cooperation opportunities.

              Coordinate internships and integrate real‑world projects into training, especially PFEs.

              Strengthen employability and foster research collaborations that align with market needs.

For Companies & Partners

              Provide a single contact point to access ENSIA students, graduates and research teams.

              Co‑organize scientific events, information days, hackathons, datathons and conferences.

              Support continuing education and joint projects tailored to industry needs.
