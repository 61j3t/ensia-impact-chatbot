[Title: Visit of National Solidarity and Family Minister]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-30T10:48:36]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence, along with its entire staff, warmly welcomed the Minister of National Solidarity and Family and her accompanying delegation of children with special needs and scouts on May 26, 2024. The school’s students made efforts to bring joy to the visiting children and put smiles on their faces.
