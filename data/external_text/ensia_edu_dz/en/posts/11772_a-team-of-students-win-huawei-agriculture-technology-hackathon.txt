[Title: A team of Students Win Huawei Agriculture Technology Hackathon]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2026-04-12T11:33:43]

A team of students from the school, consisting of Wafaa Mers, Yasmine Majri, and Doaa Jaaid, won first place at the Agriculture Technology and Environmental Protection Hackathon organized by Huawei.
Student Nesrine Bouzid was also recognized as the official representative of Algerian students in the Seeds for the Future program.
