[Title: Recruitment interviews]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-01-30T16:55:36]

Candidates for the external recruitment based on qualifications for the year 2023/2024, for positions of Assistant Professor at the National School of Artificial Intelligence, are required to attend the interview scheduled to take place at the school at 9:00 AM according to the following schedule:

Computer Science Committee: Tuesday, February 13, 2024

Mathematics Committee: Wednesday, February 14, 2024.

Candidates must bring their ID card and their official summons (available on the Progres system).

Absence on the day of the interview will result in automatic disqualification.
