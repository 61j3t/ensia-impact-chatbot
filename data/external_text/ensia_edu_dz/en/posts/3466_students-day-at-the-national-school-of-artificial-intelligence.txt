[Title: Student’s Day at The National School of Artificial Intelligence]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-05-18T09:35:39]

On the occasion of Student’s Day on May 19, our school is hosting this Thursday May, 18 a range of exciting activities for our students. One of the highlights is the presence of esteemed guest startups including Yassir, Lablabee, and Talabastore, who will be setting up their stands within the school to share their inspiring success stories with our students.
