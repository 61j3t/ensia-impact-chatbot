[Title: China-Algeria Joint Laboratory for Artificial Intelligence]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-03-12T15:54:42]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA), Algeria, and the Artificial Intelligence Research Institute of Tsinghua University (AIR), China, have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to establish the China-Algeria Joint Laboratory for Artificial Intelligence.

This partnership builds upon the Memorandum of Understanding on the Joint Construction of the China-Algeria Technology Transfer Center, signed on July 18, 2023, by the Algerian Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research and the Chinese Ministry of Science and Technology.
