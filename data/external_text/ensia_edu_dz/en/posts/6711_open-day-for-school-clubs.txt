[Title: Open Day for School Clubs]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-26T09:15:45]

The six scientific, cultural, and sports clubs of the school organized an open house on the evening of October 9, 2024, to introduce new students to their achievements in past years, their planned programs for this year, and to invite them to contribute to their success and benefit from them.
