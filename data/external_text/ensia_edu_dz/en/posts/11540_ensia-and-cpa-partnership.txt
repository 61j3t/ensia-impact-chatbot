[Title: ENSIA and CPA Partnership]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2026-02-15T15:31:46]

As part of strengthening cooperation between the academic sector and the banking sector, a partnership agreement was signed between the National School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA) and the Algerian Popular Credit (CPA).
This agreement aims to support the integration of our students into the professional environment through internships, to develop projects in the field of artificial intelligence applied to banking services, and to organize joint scientific events as well as continuing education and expertise programs.
