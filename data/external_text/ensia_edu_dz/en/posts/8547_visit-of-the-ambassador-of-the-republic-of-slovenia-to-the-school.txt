[Title: Visit of the Ambassador of the Republic of Slovenia to the School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-02-16T10:09:30]

On February 13, 2025, the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of welcoming the Ambassador of the Republic of Slovenia to Algeria, Mrs. Urška Krampurger Mandak, accompanied by the Director General of the Algerian Space Agency, Mr. Azeddine Oussedik, along with a delegation from both countries. The visitors attended presentations by the school’s students on their scientific, cultural, and sports activities. The school’s director, Mr. Abdelmalek Bachir, also gave an introductory presentation about the institution. Discussions focused on ways to strengthen scientific cooperation between the school and academic institutions in Slovenia.
