[Title: Visit by Officials to Launch Business Incubator at AI School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:19:08]

On Thursday, February 15, 2024, the National School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of receiving a visit from the Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, the delegated wali of the Sidi Abdellah administrative district, and the Director-General of the Algerian Space Agency. During the visit, a headquarters at the school was made available to the Algerian Space Agency for the creation of a joint business incubator.

The delegation also visited an exhibition for students, where members of the accredited clubs at the school and the startup organization presented displays about their various activities.
