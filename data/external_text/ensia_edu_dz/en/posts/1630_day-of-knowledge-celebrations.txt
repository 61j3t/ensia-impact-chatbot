[Title: Day of Science Celebrations]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-06-09T14:39:48]

As part of the celebration of the Science Day on 16 April 2022, the ENSIA was honoured by the visit of four ministers, Mr Abdelbaki Benziane, Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, Mr Abdelhakim Belabed, Minister of National Education, Ms Soraya Mouloudji, Minister of Culture and Arts, and Ms Kaouter Krikou, Minister of National Solidarity, Family and the Status of Women.

The day was marked by the organization of several activities:

Awards to the winners of the “
Mobile Applications Development
Hackathon” organised by the ETC scientific club at the ENSIA and the Mathematics Olympiad organised by the ENSMath.
Talks given by eminent national and international researchers on various science and technology topics.
Talks given by two students of the two schools ENSIA and ENSMath.
Visit by the ministers of the stands where the research centres CERIST, CDTA, and CRTI, as well as the student clubs exhibited the results of their work.
