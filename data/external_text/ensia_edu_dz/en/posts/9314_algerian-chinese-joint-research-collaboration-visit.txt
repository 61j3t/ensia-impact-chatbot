[Title: Algerian-Chinese Joint Research Collaboration visit]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-04-20T16:30:48]

On April 17, 2025, the National School of Artificial Intelligence had the opportunity to host the Deputy Ambassador of the People’s Republic of China to Algeria, the Director General of Scientific Research and Technological Development at the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, and representatives from research centers. Discussions focused on academic and research collaboration related to the Algerian-Chinese Joint Research Laboratory project.
