[Title: Raising Awareness About Breast Cancer in Students]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-15T11:22:43]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence, in coordination with the Public Institution for Community Health, organized an awareness campaign about breast cancer: its causes, risks, methods of prevention, detection, and treatment. The scientific clubs Skill & Tell, MedSa, and SOLEIL actively participated in this event to enhance students’ health awareness.
