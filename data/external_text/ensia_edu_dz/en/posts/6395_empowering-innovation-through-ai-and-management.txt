[Title: Empowering Innovation Through AI and Management]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-25T09:15:57]

On November 19, 2024, at the headquarters of the National School of Management, a partnership agreement was signed between the National School of Artificial Intelligence and the National School of Management. The agreement aims to strengthen collaboration and develop partnerships in various fields of common interest, such as scientific research, training, entrepreneurship, enhancing the role of university interfaces, and student clubs.
