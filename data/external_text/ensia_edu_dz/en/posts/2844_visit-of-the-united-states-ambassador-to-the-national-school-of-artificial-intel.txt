[Title: Visit of the United States Ambassador to The National School of Artificial Intelligence]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-03-07T13:51:06]

The United States Ambassador Mrs. Elizabeth Moore Aubin has visited The National School of Artificial Intelligence on Tuesday, February 14th. The visit coincided with the third day of the workshop “De-Risking and Commercialization Deep Dive” presented by researchers from The IDEA Cneter at Notre Dame University, United States.
