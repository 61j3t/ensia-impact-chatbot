[Title: Huawei ICT Academy Cooperation]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T16:50:30]

The Huawei ICT Academy is a joint program between Huawei and ENSAI (École Nationale Supérieure d’intiligence artificial) in Algeria. The goal of the program is to provide a better understanding of the digital transformation in the ICT industry and to train students to be able to work in this field.
