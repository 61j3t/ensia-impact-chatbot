[Title: Collaboration on AI  With University of Ljubljana in Slovenia]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-29T09:34:57]

As part of implementing the cooperation agreement between the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research and the Slovenian Ministry of Digital Transformation in the field of artificial intelligence, a scientific meeting was held today, June 4, 2024, via remote communication technologies. The meeting brought together the National School of Artificial Intelligence and the University of Ljubljana in Slovenia. It included presentations by professors from both sides, highlighting their research work and exploring ways to establish joint research projects.
