[Title: Visit of the Mauritanian Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research to the School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-02-18T11:42:32]

On February 16, 2025, the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of welcoming Mr. Yaqub Ould Ameen, Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania. He was accompanied by the Mauritanian ambassador to Algeria, along with officials from his country. On the Algerian side, the delegation was joined by Mr. Abdelhakim Ben Tlis, Secretary General of the Algerian Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, along with a group of ministry officials. The visit aimed to learn about the school’s activities and discuss ways of cooperation between Mauritanian academic institutions and the school in the field of education.
