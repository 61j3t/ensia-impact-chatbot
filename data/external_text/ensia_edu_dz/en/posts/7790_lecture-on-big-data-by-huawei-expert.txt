[Title: Lecture on Big Data by Huawei Expert]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:00:28]

Students of the National School of Artificial Intelligence and some of its professors attended a lecture today, February 27, 2024, by Mr. Li Chan Zhong, a big data expert in government sectors and public security from Huawei. The lecture was followed by a discussion involving students, professors, and the speaker.
