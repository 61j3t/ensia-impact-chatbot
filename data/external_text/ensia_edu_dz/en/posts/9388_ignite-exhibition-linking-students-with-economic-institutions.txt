[Title: IGNITE Exhibition: Linking Students with Economic Institutions]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-04-27T09:32:50]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence, located in the scientific and technological hub of Sidi Abdallah, hosted today, Saturday, April 26, 2025, the “IGNITE” exhibition. The event was organized by the Entrepreneurship and Business Club “EBEC” of the school and sponsored by “CASH Assurance”.
Forty-five economic institutions participated in the exhibition, including large national groups, small and medium-sized enterprises, and several start-ups.

The exhibition aims to:

Introduce students to active institutions in the field of artificial intelligence and strengthen their ability to communicate with them.

Promote networking between students and school professors on one side, and economic institutions on the other.

Train students in job interview skills and CV preparation.

Open opportunities for internships and job offers to students.

Inspire students with ideas to create their own start-up projects or propose scientific research topics based on market needs.

The “IGNITE” exhibition served as a practical platform to bring closer the higher education sector and the economic sector.
