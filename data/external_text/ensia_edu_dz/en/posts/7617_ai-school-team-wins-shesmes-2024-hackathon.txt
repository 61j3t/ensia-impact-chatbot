[Title: AI School Team Wins SHESMES 2024 Hackathon]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-31T10:21:45]

A team of students from the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence achieved first place after representing their school and participating in the first edition of the “Student Hackathon for Environmental, Social, and Economic Modernization” – SHESMES 2024. The event was organized by Oran 1 University Ahmed Ben Bella under the theme “Digitizing Sustainable Development,” coinciding with the second edition of the scientific clubs meeting on sustainable development.
Congratulations to the students and the school.
