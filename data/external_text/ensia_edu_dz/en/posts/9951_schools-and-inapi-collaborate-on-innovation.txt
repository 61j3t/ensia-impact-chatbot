[Title: Schools and INAPI Collaborate on Innovation]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-05-26T10:45:44]

On the morning of Thursday, May 22, 2025, the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence, along with the National Higher School of Mathematics and the National Higher School of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology, signed partnership agreements with the Algerian National Institute of Industrial Property (INAPI), with the aim of establishing Technology and Innovation Support Centers (CATI) within these schools.

These centers will assist students in protecting their innovations and will organize practical training workshops on the concepts and principles of industrial property.

On this occasion, Mr. Belmehdi Abdelhafid gave an informative presentation about the institute’s missions and the procedures for registering and protecting industrial innovations.
