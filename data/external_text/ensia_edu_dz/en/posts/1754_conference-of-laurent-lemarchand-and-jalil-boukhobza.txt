[Title: Conference by Dr Laurent LeMarchand and Prof Jalil Boukhobza]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-25T10:34:14]

As part of the regular seminars held at the school, the ENSIA was pleased to welcome on April 19, 2022, Dr.
 Laurent LeMarchand
 and
Prof Jalil Boukhobza
 from the University of Bretagne Occidentale, France.

Dr. Le Marchand presented the methods and applications of multi-objective combinatorial optimization methods.

Prof. Boukhobza presented a talk titled: Memory technologies and data storage: from Mesopotamian tablets to Google servers.
