[Title: Open days at the National School of Artificial Intelligence – July 17-20, 2022]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-03-07T13:38:10]

The first day of the open days witnessed a turnout by the new baccalaureate holders to inquire about how to enroll in the school and the conditions for admission. The school staff answered all questions raised by prospective students who are given introductory brochures about the National School of Artificial Intelligence.

The open days in the National School of Artificial Intelligence continued for the second day in a row, where the school was honored to welcome a large number of students and parents who were provided with all the relevant information about the school. Further, the procedures related to the initial and final registration are explained.
The third day of the open days was special for students as they obtained their baccalaureate transcripts showing the weighted marks. Students who visited the school asked more accurate questions regarding the admission into the National School of Artificial Intelligence whilst the answers were clearer.
