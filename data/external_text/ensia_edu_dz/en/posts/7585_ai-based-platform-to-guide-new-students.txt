[Title: AI-Based Platform to Guide New Students]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-31T10:16:52]

Under the supervision of the Minister of Higher Education, Mr. Kamel Baddari, the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research launched a platform to assist new baccalaureate students in their choices. The platform was developed in collaboration between the National School of Artificial Intelligence and the ministry, by students from the school under the supervision of a professor from the school and the ministry’s digitalization director. The platform uses artificial intelligence and data mining technologies.
