[Title: Visit from the National Institute of Nigeria]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-30T10:41:42]

As part of a scientific visit focused on artificial intelligence, security, and economics—challenges and prospects—the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of hosting a delegation of 25 trainees and supervisors from the National Institute for Security Studies of Nigeria, led by Mr. Kareem Adigboyega Adebayo. The school’s director and administrative team welcomed the guests and introduced them to the school’s facilities. The director also gave a presentation outlining the school’s objectives, educational programs, and local and international agreements and partnerships.
