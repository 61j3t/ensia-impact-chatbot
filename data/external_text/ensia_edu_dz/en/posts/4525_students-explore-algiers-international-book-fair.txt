[Title: Students Explore Algiers International Book Fair]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-11-05T12:57:29]

On Saturday, October 28, 2023, students from the school, accompanied by a team of employees with the library director, visited Algiers International Book Fair to explore the wide range of books and content on display for possible acquisitions for the school’s library.
