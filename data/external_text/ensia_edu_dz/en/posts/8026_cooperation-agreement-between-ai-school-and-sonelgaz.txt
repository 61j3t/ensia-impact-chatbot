[Title: Cooperation Agreement Between AI School and Sonelgaz]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:37:57]

On December 25, 2023, a cooperation agreement was signed between the National School of Artificial Intelligence and the Algerian Electricity and Gas Company (Sonelgaz). The aim of this agreement is to:

Establish and manage a joint business incubator at the school to develop and monitor innovative projects.

Organize training courses and internships.

Organize scientific meetings.

Support and guide students.

Share the use of data and resources (including computational capabilities) and exchange research results carried out by both parties (market studies, technical feasibility studies, etc.).
