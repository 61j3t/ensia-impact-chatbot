[Title: Scientific Club GDG Concludes Technopoly 2025 Event]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-02-13T14:38:33]

The scientific club of the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence, GDG, concluded its scientific event
Technopoly
, which took place from February 6 to 8, 2025. The event focused on finding solutions to various problems related to technical, commercial, and marketing aspects. It was organized in partnership with the
Excellence EHEC
 scientific club.
