[Title: Aymen Zahzouh Excels at the National Kempo Championship]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-11-09T12:17:22]

Aymen Zahzouh, a student and elite athlete from the National School of Artificial Intelligence, earned one gold medal and two bronze medals in the National Kempo Championship. The competition was held from October 30 to November 1, 2025, at the multipurpose sports hall in Cherchell.
