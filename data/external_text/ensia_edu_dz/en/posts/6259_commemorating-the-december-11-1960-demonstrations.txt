[Title: Commemorating the        December 11, 1960 Demonstrations]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-14T09:25:00]

On the occasion of the anniversary of the December 11, 1960 demonstrations, the school organized various activities, including a book exhibition featuring works on Algeria’s history, as well as observing a minute of silence in tribute to the martyrs.
