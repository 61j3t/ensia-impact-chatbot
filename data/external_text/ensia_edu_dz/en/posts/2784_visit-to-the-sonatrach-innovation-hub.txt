[Title: Visit to the Sonatrach Innovation Hub]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-03-07T13:56:37]

The school has organised a visit to its partner Sonatrach’s Innovation Hub, on Thursday January 26th.

The hub team prepared a series of presentations for the students about the hub, its role, ecosystem and strategies, but also about their datacenter infrastructure and technologies.

The visit was concluded with a tour to the datacenter.
