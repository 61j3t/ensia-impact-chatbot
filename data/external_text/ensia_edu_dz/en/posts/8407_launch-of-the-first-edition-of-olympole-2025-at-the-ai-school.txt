[Title: Launch of the first edition of OLYMPOLE 2025 at the AI School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-02-10T08:24:59]

The OLYMPOLE event, in its first edition, was inaugurated yesterday, February 8, 2025, at the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence. The event is organized by the school’s scientific club, ESC, and will continue until February 28, 2025, featuring a diverse scientific, cultural, and sports program.
