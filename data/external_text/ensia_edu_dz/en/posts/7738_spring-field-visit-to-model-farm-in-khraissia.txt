[Title: Spring Field Visit to Model Farm in Khraissia]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T08:52:02]

Since spring is a season for celebrating the environment and its diversity, on March 2, 2024, students from the National School of Artificial Intelligence organized a scientific, cultural, and recreational field visit to the model farm located in Khraissia. They were warmly welcomed by the specialized staff, who introduced them to environmental aspects and the risks threatening them, while also guiding them through the farm’s various areas.
