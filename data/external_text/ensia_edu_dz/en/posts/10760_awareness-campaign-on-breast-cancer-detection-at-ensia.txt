[Title: Awareness Campaign on Breast Cancer Detection at ENSIA]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-10-30T09:01:49]

As part of the global Breast Cancer Awareness Month,
Pink October
, the National School of Artificial Intelligence hosted on October 23, 2025, in coordination with the Public Health Institution of Zeralda, doctors from the university residences in the area, and the scientific clubs of the following institutions: the National School of Artificial Intelligence, the University of Health Sciences – Youssef El-Khatib, and the University of Science and Technology – Houari Boumediene, an awareness campaign about this disease and methods of detection.
