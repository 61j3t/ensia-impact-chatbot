[Title: Exploring AI Applications in Oil and Gas Sector]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:25:19]

As part of the efforts to open higher education and research institutions to their economic and social environment, the National School of Artificial Intelligence organized an open day on February 12, 2024, with the presence of some economic and social partners (Sonatrach, InnoDevData, Qareeb). Representatives from Sonatrach gave a lecture on the future of artificial intelligence applications in the oil and gas sector. Additionally, presentations and meetings were held by representatives from the three companies with the school’s students.
