[Title: Visit of Professor Timothy Peoples]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T18:22:00]

Professor
Timothy People
, a fulbright specialist and a professor at Purdue University, USA, visited the ENSIA as part of the cooperation agreement between the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research and the Ambassy of the USA in Algeria. During his visit that lasted a month, Prof Peoples gave several talk on entrepreneurship to students and had meetings with the school’s faculty members and management staff and worked together for the development of a rich and topical programme around entrepreneurship. During his stay in Algiers, Prof Peoples also visited various research centres and higher education schools to learn about the local ecosystem with the goal of propose content adaption to the Algerian context.
