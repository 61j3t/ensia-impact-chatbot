[Title: Visit of th president of The National Committee for the Promotion of Visibility and Ranking of Higher Education Institutions to the school]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-02-03T09:05:22]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of hosting the President of the National Committee for the Promotion of Visibility and Ranking of Higher Education Institutions and his accompanying team. They gave a detailed presentation on improving the rankings of the two schools (Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics) in global classifications. The local committees of both schools, as well as the directors of the two schools, attended the meeting.
