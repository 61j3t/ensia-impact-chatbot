[Title: Start of Field Internships at Algerian Space Agency]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-09-23T10:46:50]

On the occasion of the start of the field internships for fourth-year students, a workshop was organized on Sunday, September 7, 2025, at the Algerian Space Agency. The workshop welcomed 38 new interns and included a presentation on the activities and tasks they will be assigned during their internship period.

The workshop began with a speech by Mr. Azzedine Oussedik, Director General of the Algerian Space Agency, who welcomed the students and emphasized that the agency’s doors are open to them. He encouraged them to make the most of this practical experience by applying their knowledge and skills in line with Algeria’s objectives in the fields of space and advanced technology.

Mr. Abdelmalek Bachir, Director of the School, also gave a speech in which he underlined the importance of these internships in helping students strengthen their theoretical knowledge through practical experience, especially in the context of real projects. He highlighted the effective cooperation between the School and the Algerian Space Agency as an example of complementarity between academic training and practical application.

The program of the workshop also included a detailed presentation by officials and experts from the agency. It covered the different projects and technical and scientific tasks in which the students will be involved, as well as practical guidance to support their smooth integration into the agency’s work environment.
