[Title: visit to the Algiers Book Fair]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-08T09:13:23]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence, in coordination with the Directorate of University Services – West Algiers, organized a cultural visit to the Algiers International Book Fair on Friday, November 15, 2024, for some of the school’s students accompanied by a few of their professors. The visit aimed to explore recent scientific publications and acquire available books.
