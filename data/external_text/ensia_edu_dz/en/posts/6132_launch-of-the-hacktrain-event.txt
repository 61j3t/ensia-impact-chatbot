[Title: Launch of the Hack&Train Event]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-08T08:56:22]

On the evening of December 19, 2024, at the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence, the Hack&Train hackathon was launched. Participating teams will work on solving problems related to the Algerian Space Agency. The event is organized by the school’s scientific club, SecAI, in collaboration with the Algerian Space Agency (ASAL) and sponsored by CASH Assurances.
