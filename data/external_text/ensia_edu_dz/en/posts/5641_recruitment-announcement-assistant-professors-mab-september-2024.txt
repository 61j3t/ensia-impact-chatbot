Application Requirements:

The application must include the following documents:

A handwritten application letter,

A fully completed information sheet. The information sheet can be downloaded from the website
www.concours-fonction-publique.gov.dz
,

A copy of the national identity card

A copy of the required degree or an equivalent recognized title

A copy of documents proving the candidate’s scientific work in their field and specialization before or after obtaining the required degree (copies of articles and scientific papers, as well as a copy of the ISBN page of scientific books if applicable)

Work certificates proving the professional experience in higher education establishments, if applicable (to be downloaded from the relevant university website)

Work certificates proving professional experience in other sectors after obtaining the required degree, along with a certificate of social security affiliation

Work certificates proving professional experience in management positions in public institutions or administrations, along with a certificate of social security affiliation

For candidates who are definitively accepted in the competition, the following additional documents must be submitted before appointment:

Copy of the national service status document

Valid residence certificate

Birth certificate extract

Two (2) medical certificates (general medicine and physiology) from a specialist doctor certifying fitness for the position

Two (2) identity photos

Family record, if applicable

A copy of the disability card, if applicable

Application deadline:
The registration period for the competition via the “PROGRES” platform is open for 15 working days starting from the first publication of the announcement in the press.

Failure to attend the interview on the scheduled date and time will result in exclusion from the competition.

Candidates can check the results of their application for the Assistant Professor position through the “PROGRES” platform using their electronic account, once the results are published by the relevant institution.

Note:
The interviews for selected candidates will take place at the National School of Artificial Intelligence in Sidi Abdellah, Algiers.
