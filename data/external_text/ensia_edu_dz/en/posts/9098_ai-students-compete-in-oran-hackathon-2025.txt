[Title: AI Students Compete in Oran Hackathon 2025]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-04-10T08:46:49]

Two teams from the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence participated on April 6, 7, and 8, 2025, in the second edition of the Student Hackathon for Environmental, Social, and Economic Modernization, organized by Oran 1 University Ahmed Ben Bella. The school’s team, BROOTIES, achieved first place.
