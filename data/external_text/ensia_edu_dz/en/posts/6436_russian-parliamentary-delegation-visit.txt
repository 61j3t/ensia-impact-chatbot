[Title: Russian parliamentary delegation visit]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-15T11:07:09]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor today, October 31, 2024, of hosting a Russian parliamentary delegation led by the Deputy Speaker of the State Duma of the Russian Federation, Vladislav Davenkov. The visit was attended by the Wali Delegate of the Sidi Abdallah Province, along with Algerian parliamentarians. The Director of the school presented an introductory presentation about the school and its activities.
