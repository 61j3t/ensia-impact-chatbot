[Title: Partnership and Cooperation Agreement between ENSIA and ENSGDT]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-11-05T09:58:05]

“On October 26, 2023, a formal partnership and cooperation agreement was established, focusing on pedagogical and research collaboration, between the National School of Artificial Intelligence and the National School of Geodetic Sciences and Space Technologies.”
