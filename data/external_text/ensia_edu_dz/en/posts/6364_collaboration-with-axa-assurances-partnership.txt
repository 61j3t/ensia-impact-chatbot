[Title: Collaboration with AXA Assurances Partnership]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-14T09:42:58]

On November 25, 2024, a cooperation agreement was signed with AXA Assurances Algérie, a company specialized in insurance. The agreement aims to:

Provide training and internships.

Organize scientific events.

Support students.
