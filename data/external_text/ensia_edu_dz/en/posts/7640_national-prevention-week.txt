[Title: National Prevention Week]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-31T10:29:28]

As part of the National Prevention Week under the theme “A Healthy Lifestyle for Everyone,” the National School of Artificial Intelligence, in coordination with the university campus schools and residences, raised awareness among students about: the proper use of medications, prevention and combating addiction, healthy and balanced nutrition, risk factor prevention, and promoting physical activities. The school’s ESCC club also participated, with its health culture officer in attendance.
