[Title: Slovenian Diplomat Visits School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:29:20]

The school had the honor of receiving Mr. Boqdan Patic, the Chargé d’Affaires of the Embassy of the Republic of Slovenia in Algeria. He was welcomed by the school director and the assistant directors in charge of education and international relations. The possibility of collaboration in training and scientific research in the field of artificial intelligence was discussed, along with the potential for establishing partnership agreements with universities and research centers in Slovenia.
