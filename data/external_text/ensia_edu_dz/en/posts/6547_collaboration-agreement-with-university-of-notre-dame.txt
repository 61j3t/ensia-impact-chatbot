[Title: Collaboration Agreement with University of Notre Dame]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-15T11:16:40]

The directors of the two national schools in Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics, along with the University of Notre Dame du Lac in the United States, signed an agreement at the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research. The agreement aims to promote collaboration in scientific and pedagogical research. This took place during a visit by a delegation from the University of Notre Dame to the two schools to conduct workshops and give lectures.
The signing ceremony was attended by the Secretary General of the Ministry, the U.S. Ambassador to Algeria, and the Director General of the U.S. Department of State’s American Affairs Division.
This agreement reflects the Algeria_research_hub project, initiated by the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research and the U.S. Embassy in Algeria three years ago. The project brings together the National School of Artificial Intelligence, the National School of Mathematics, and the University of Notre Dame in the United States.
