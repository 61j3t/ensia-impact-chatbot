[Title: Students Visit Huawei Algeria]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-07-07T08:14:08]

As part of the cooperation agreement signed between the National School of Artificial Intelligence and Huawei Algeria, a group of students from the school visited Huawei Algeria on July 3, 2025. The visit included an introduction to the company’s departments, its fields of work, and its future strategies. It also gave the students an opportunity to express their academic and research concerns as well as their professional aspirations.
