[Title: ENSIA Tech Community’s Excursion to the Advanced Technology Development Center CDTA]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-03-07T14:02:40]

The ENSIA Tech Community club has organized an excursion to the Advanced Technology Development Center (CDTA). The group has visited the center’s different departments and discovered their main research fields and activities, namely the cleanroom where integrated circuits (ICs) are manufactured, the augmented/virtual reality (AR/VR) laboratories, the robotics department and finally the laboratory for sensors prototyping and fabrication. This trip’s main    aim was to prepare our students for future collaborations in similar research projects.
