[Title: Hackathon to Provide Solutions that Improve Freelance Project Evaluation System]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-02-24T14:47:41]

Nine teams of students from the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence participated in a hackathon on February 21-22, 2025. The event aimed to develop a mechanism for filtering proposals for freelance activity projects for the Ministry of Micro-Enterprises, Startups, and the Knowledge Economy. The Minister of the sector, Mr. Wadhah Nour-Eddine, attended the event and listened to the teams’ presentations. The teams submitted their results at the end of February 22, and a special committee is currently reviewing and evaluating the proposed solutions. The successful teams will be announced after the evaluation process is completed.
