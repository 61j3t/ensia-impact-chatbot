[Title: Visit of His Excellency the Ambassador of Iraq and His Excellency the Ambassador of Yemen]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T17:19:41]

The ENSIA was honored by the visit of His Excellency the Ambassador of Yemen and His Excellency the Ambassador of Iraq to Algeria. Their Excellencies were jointly welcomed by Prof Ahmed Guessoum, Acting Director of the ENSIA, and Prof Hacene Belbachir, Acting Director of the ENSMath. After a visit to the premises, Their Excellencies had discussions with the schools directors on possible scientific collaborations with their respective home universities.
