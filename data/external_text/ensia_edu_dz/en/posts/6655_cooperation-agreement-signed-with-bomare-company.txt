[Title: Cooperation Agreement Signed with Bomare Company]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-25T14:21:12]

On October 13, 2024, a cooperation agreement was signed with Bomare Company, specialized in the manufacturing of electronic devices. This agreement aims to:

Support innovative projects.

Provide training and internships.

Organize scientific events.
