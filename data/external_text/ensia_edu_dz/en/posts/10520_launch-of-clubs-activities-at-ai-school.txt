[Title: Launch of Clubs’ Activities at AI  School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-10-02T13:45:35]

On October 1, 2025, the scientific clubs of the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence launched their scientific, cultural, and sports activities for the 2025–2026 academic year. The opening included an exhibition showcasing the activities of previous years and a presentation of the program for the current year.
