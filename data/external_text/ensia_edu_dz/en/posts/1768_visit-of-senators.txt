[Title: Visit of Senators]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T17:24:39]

The ENSIA was honored by a visit from members of the Commission for Education, Training, Higher Education, Scientific Research and Religious Affairs of the Council of the Nation (the Senate). After a short explanation by the director of the school, the members of the commission visited the school’s facilities. They had a discussion with the students about the motivation behind the creation of this school as well as the challenges that await them to promote the use and development of artificial intelligence in Algeria.
