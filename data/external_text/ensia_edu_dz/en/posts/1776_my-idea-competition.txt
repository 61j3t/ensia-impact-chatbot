[Title: My Idea Competition]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-03-07T13:46:05]

In partnership with the
ANVREDET
, the
DGRSDT
 and the World Intellectual Property Organisation (Algeria Office), the ENSIA organized a competition titled
My IDEA
of more than 60 hours of initiation to innovation led by a team of coaches from the ANVREDET. A conference titled:
Artificial Intelligence and Innovation
, was presented during the event by the ANVREDET team. At the end of the competition, prizes were awarded to the three winning teams.
