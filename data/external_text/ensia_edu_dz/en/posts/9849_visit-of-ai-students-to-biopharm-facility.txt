[Title: Visit of AI Students to Biopharm Facility]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-05-25T15:45:37]

On May 21, 2025, a group of students from the National School of Artificial Intelligence visited the Biopharm industrial facility. They were welcomed by the staff, who introduced them to the various departments and areas of specialization. The students were also given an explanation of the drug manufacturing process, from assembling the raw materials to production and marketing. The visit also highlighted the facility’s need for artificial intelligence in managing and modernizing its growing database.
