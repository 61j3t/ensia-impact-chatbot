[Title: ENSIA and GAM Assurances Partnership Agreement]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2026-05-19T10:40:47]

As part of strengthening cooperation between the academic sector and the insurance sector, a partnership agreement was signed between the National School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA) and GAM Assurances.

This agreement aims to support the integration of our students into the professional environment by providing internship and training opportunities, encouraging the development of innovative projects in the field of artificial intelligence applied to insurance services, as well as organizing joint scientific events, continuing education programs, and exchanges of expertise.
