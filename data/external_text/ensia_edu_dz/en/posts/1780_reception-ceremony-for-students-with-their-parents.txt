[Title: Reception ceremony for students with their parents]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T18:37:47]

The École Nationale Supérieure d’Intelligence Artificielle organised a reception ceremony for new students and their parents on October 10th. The reception was an opportunity for students and parents to discover the school and its teaching and administrative staff.
