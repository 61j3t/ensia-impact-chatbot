[Title: Health Awareness Event]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-14T08:58:23]

On December 12, 2024, the National School of Artificial Intelligence hosted a health-focused event aimed at promoting awareness and fostering community well-being. The program featured:

AIDS Awareness Campaign
: Informing students about the risks, prevention, and impact of AIDS.

Blood Donation Education
: Highlighting the life-saving importance of blood donation and encouraging student participation.

Basic First Aid Training
: Equipping participants with essential first aid skills to respond effectively in emergencies.

This impactful event was organized in collaboration with the Public Health Establishment of Zalda, the National Blood Agency, the Algerian Red Crescent (Mahelma branch), and the school’s Sports and Cultural Club (ESCC). It underscored the school’s commitment to health education and community engagement.
