[Title: ENSIA Students Achieve Top Ranks at Innov Tech]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-11-26T10:21:51]

Several teams of students from the National School of Artificial Intelligence took part in the national innovation hackathon
Innov Tech
 from November 20 to 23, 2025. The event focused on digital creation and advanced uses of 5G and artificial intelligence technologies in Algeria. The school’s teams achieved the following rankings:

First place: RA9MANA.

Second place: THE REELERS + THINK AI.

Third place: PERCEPTRONAT + THE FIRELIGHTS.

The hackathon was held at the Scientific and Technological Hub “Martyr Abdelhafid Ihdadden” in Sidi Abdellah, in partnership between the National Agency for Research Results Development and Djezzy. The solutions developed will be supported to become startup projects.
