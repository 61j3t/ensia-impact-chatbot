[Title: Administration Board Meeting]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-12-24T11:09:03]

The Administration Board of the National School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA) met on December 19, 2023, at 2:00 PM, chaired by Professor Djamal-Eddine Akretche, with the presence of the members of the board. The agenda of the meeting was:

The report of activities and expenditures for the year 2023

The budget project for the year 2024

The student internal regulations

During the session, the board members engaged in an insightful discussion regarding the activities report, which was presented by the school’s director, Professor Abdelmalik Bachir. The presentation shed light on various developments and challenges encountered in 2023, such as the successful transition to a new building, the notable increase in student enrollment, and the ongoing recruitment efforts. Subsequently, discussions ensued regarding the agenda elements, resulting in the board’s approval.
