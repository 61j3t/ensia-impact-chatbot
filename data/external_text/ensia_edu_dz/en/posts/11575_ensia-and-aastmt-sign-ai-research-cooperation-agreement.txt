[Title: ENSIA and AASTMT Sign AI Research Cooperation Agreement]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2026-02-15T15:58:01]

We are pleased to announce that the National School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA) and the Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) signed a memorandum of understanding on January 28, 2026, to strengthen scientific and research cooperation in the field of artificial intelligence. This strategic partnership aims to build bridges of collaboration between the two institutions through: the exchange of students, faculty, and researchers; the development of joint research projects and studies; the exchange of curricula, publications, and scientific documents; and the joint organization of scientific and technical events and seminars.
