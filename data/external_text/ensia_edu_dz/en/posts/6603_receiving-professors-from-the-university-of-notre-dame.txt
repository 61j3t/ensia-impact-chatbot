[Title: Receiving professors from the University of Notre Dame]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-15T14:51:58]

As part of the Algeria research hub project, which brings together the National School of Artificial Intelligence and the National School of Mathematics with the University of Notre Dame in the United States, professors from the University of Notre Dame were hosted, and workshops and lectures were organized for students on October 20, 22, and 23.
