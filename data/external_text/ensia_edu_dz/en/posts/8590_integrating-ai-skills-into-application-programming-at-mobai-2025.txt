[Title: 2025 MobAi Scientific event]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-02-18T11:18:45]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence hosted the scientific event MobAI over three days, from February 13 to 15, 2025. This event is part of the annual program of the school’s scientific club, Skill & Tell. It focused on ways to integrate application programming with artificial intelligence skills.
