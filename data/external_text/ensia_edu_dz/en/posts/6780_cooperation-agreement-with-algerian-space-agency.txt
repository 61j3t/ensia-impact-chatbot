[Title: Cooperation Agreement with Algerian Space Agency]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-26T14:03:15]

On September 12, 2024, a framework agreement was signed between the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence and the Algerian Space Agency, aiming to:

Support projects

Provide training and internships

Organize scientific events

Facilitate employment and guidance
