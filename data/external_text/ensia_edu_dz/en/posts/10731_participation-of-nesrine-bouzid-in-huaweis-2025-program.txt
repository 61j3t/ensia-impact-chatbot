[Title: Participation of Nesrine Bouzid in Huawei’s 2025 Program]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-10-30T08:40:16]

Nesrine Bouzid, a student at the National School of Artificial Intelligence, took part in the
Seeds for the Future 2025
 program from October 18 to 26, organized by Huawei. She was among the 20 students selected at the national level. The program included a combination of technical training in 5G technologies, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and more, as well as sessions on personal and leadership skills, in addition to a cultural exploration of China and visits to major companies there.
