[Title: Commemorating National Immigration Day]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-16T10:48:35]

On the occasion of National Immigration Day on October 17, the school commemorated this event by organizing a book exhibition on the history of Algeria and observing a minute of silence in memory of the martyrs.
