[Title: Visit of Business Manager at the Russian Embassy to the School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-24T13:47:01]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of receiving a visit this morning, December 9, 2024, from Mr. Kassai Koushishkov, the Chargé d’Affaires at the Russian Embassy in Algeria. The meeting involved an exchange of views on ways to develop cooperation between the school and Russian higher education institutions in the fields of education and training, particularly in artificial intelligence.
