[Title: Students of AI School Visit CRAAG]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:43:34]

On the evening of Tuesday, December 20, 2023, students from the National School of Artificial Intelligence visited the Center for Research in Astronomy, Astrophysics, and Geophysics (CRAAG) for a cultural visit. The students toured various departments of the center, accompanied by researchers and specialists who informed them about the center’s various functions and services, with a detailed explanation of the equipment and tools used and how they operate.
