[Title: Administration Board Meeting]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-08T08:50:21]

On Wednesday, December 18, 2024, at 9:30 a.m., a meeting of the Board of Directors of the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence was held at the school’s headquarters, chaired by Mr. Jamal Eddine Akrache and attended by the members of the council. The meeting included the presentation and discussion of the activity report for 2024, the budget proposal for 2024, as well as the discussion of the budget proposal for 2025, which was unanimously approved.
