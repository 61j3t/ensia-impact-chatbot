[Title: Students Compete in National Robotics and Coding Event]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-04-16T10:38:26]

To mark the occasion of Knowledge Day on April 16, the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence took part in the National University Programming and Robotics Competition, held at the University of Biskra from April 12 to 15, 2025. Representing the school, a team of students showcased their skills and dedication throughout the competition. Their efforts were crowned with success, as one team member earned the Best Programmer award – a proud achievement for both the student and the institution.
