[Title: Hack&Train Event Conclusion]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-07T09:10:38]

The activities of the Hack & Train Hackathon concluded yesterday evening, December 21, 2024, at the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence. The winning teams in the top three positions for intermediates and advanced participants were honored. The efforts of the SecAi scientific club, the event organizer, were celebrated, and thanks were extended to the Algerian Space Agency (ASAL), the event’s partner, and the official sponsor, CASH Assurances.
