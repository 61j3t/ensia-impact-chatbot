[Title: Cybersecurity Training for AI School Students]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T08:56:25]

A group of students from the National School of Artificial Intelligence benefited from a three-day training course in the field of cybersecurity and antivirus software. The course was conducted by experts from Dr.WEB, a company specialized in this field since 1992. The company also awarded certificates to the participating students.
