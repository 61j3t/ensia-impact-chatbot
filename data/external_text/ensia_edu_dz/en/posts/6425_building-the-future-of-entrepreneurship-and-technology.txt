[Title: Building the Future of Entrepreneurship and Technology]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-15T11:01:46]

On November 3, 2024, an agreement was signed between the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence and the National Agency for Support and Development of Entrepreneurship concerning the Entrepreneurship Development Center at the school. This agreement was made in collaboration with the National Higher School of Mathematics and the National Higher School of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology.
