[Title: Design Thinking Workshop for Students]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:04:03]

A workshop on “design thinking” was held today at the school for third-year students, with contributions from the “Group Project” course instructors.
The aim of this workshop is to enable them to solve problems in an innovative way.
