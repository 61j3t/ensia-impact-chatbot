[Title: Visit of Her Excellency the  Ambassador of Turkey]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T17:03:10]

The ENSIA was honored by the visit of Her Excellency the Ambassador of Turkey, Mahinur Ozdemir Goktas. Her Excellency was welcomed by Prof Abdelmalik Bachir, Director of the ENSIA and Prof Hacene Belbachir, Acting Director of the ENSMath.  Her Excellency was given information about the two schools and discussed possible scientific collaborations with Turkish universities.
