[Title: Victory Day Commemoration]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-31T10:42:44]

On the occasion of Victory Day, an exhibition was held at the National School of Artificial Intelligence featuring some historical books on the Algerian Liberation Revolution, the struggle of the Algerian people against colonial occupation, and the Evian negotiations that led to the ceasefire on March 19, 1962.
