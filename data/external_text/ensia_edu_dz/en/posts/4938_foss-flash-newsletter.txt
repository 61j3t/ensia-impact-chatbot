ENSIA Tech Community (ETC), one of the scientific clubs in the school, proudly announces the launch of the
FOSS Flash Project
, a
bi-weekly
 email newsletter aimed at promoting the use of
Free and Open Source Software
 among students in favor of proprietary ones.

The subscribers of the newsletter expect to receive an article every 2 weeks, in which they find detailed description of a Free and Open Source software along with reasons and resources to encourage switching to it in favor of its proprietary peers.

You can join this journey today! by subscribing through their website:
ETC Newsletter
, and let ETC members guide you to a liberated digital experience. Just type your email, click subscribe, and you're in!

Stay inspired, stay ETC! 🔥
