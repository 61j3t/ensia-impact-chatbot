[Title: Open Days for New AI School Students]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-09-23T11:04:05]

As part of the 2025–2026 academic year and the reception of new students of the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence, the school organized open days on September 20 and 21, 2025, through its various departments and scientific clubs, to answer the new students’ questions, address their concerns, and provide guidance.
