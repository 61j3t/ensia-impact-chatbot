[Title: The Large Language Model Revolution: Highlights from Pr. Debbah’s Talk]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-04-26T09:41:58]

Yesterday, April 25, 2023, our school had the privilege of hosting Professor Merouane Debbah, a chief researcher at the Technology Innovation Institute (TII), Abu Dhabi. The talk focused on large language models (LLMs) like Noor and GPT-4, and it was attended by a large number of students and teachers.

During the talk, Professor Debbah discussed the latest developments in natural language processing (NLP) and how these models are transforming various industries. He also provided insights into the challenges and opportunities associated with large language models, including their potential impact on the society.

The event was very interactive, with many students and teachers asking inspiring questions and engaging in lively discussions with Professor Debbah. In addition, the event was broadcasted live on the school’s
Facebook
 page, allowing people who couldn’t attend to participate in the event.

We are grateful to Professor Debbah for sharing his expertise and insights with our school’s community, and we look forward to hosting more events like this in the future.
