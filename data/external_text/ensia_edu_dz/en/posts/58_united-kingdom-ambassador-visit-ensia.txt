[Title: Visit of Her Excellency the Ambassador of the UK]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T17:58:56]

The ENSIA was honored by the visit of Her Excellency the Ambassador of the United Kingdom to Algeria, Mrs. Sharon Wardle. Her Excellency visited the extensive languages teaching labs and spoke to a group of students. Her Excellency had also discussions with the directors of the two schools ENSIA and ENSMath on the training provided and on possible collaborations with universities in the United Kingdom.
