[Title: Student Visit to ICOSNET]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-05-25T10:24:25]

On May 22, 2025, a group of students from the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence visited the ICOSNET company in response to their invitation.
During the visit, the students learned about the different departments of the company, including technical aspects, databases, clients, and marketing. This gave them a useful opportunity to connect theoretical knowledge with practical applications in the fields of technology and communications.
