[Title: Awareness Day on Drug Prevention at AI School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:08:30]

On February 20, 2024, the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence organized an awareness day on combating drugs and addiction in the university environment. The event was attended by two specialist teams from the Algerian Red Crescent and the Zeralda Public Community Health Institution, with notable student engagement.
