[Title: ENSIA and USS Sign AI Health Agreement]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2026-02-15T15:41:32]

A cooperation agreement was signed between the National School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA) and the University of Health Sciences (USS) to strengthen scientific research and training in the field of artificial intelligence applied to health.
This partnership aims to support innovation, develop intelligent medical solutions, and train skilled professionals capable of contributing to the future of digital health in Algeria.
