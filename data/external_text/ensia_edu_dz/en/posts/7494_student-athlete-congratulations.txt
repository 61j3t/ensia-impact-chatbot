[Title: Student Athlete Congratulations]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-30T11:10:17]

The Director of the National School of Artificial Intelligence, congratulated the student-athlete Aymen Zahzouh for his outstanding sports performance during his participation with the national team in the 22nd edition of the World Kempo Championship held in Antalya, Turkey, where he won a silver medal and two bronze medals. On the occasion of the inaugural day of the school’s cultural and sports club, the director also delivered a speech encouraging students to actively engage in various scientific, cultural, and sports activities.
