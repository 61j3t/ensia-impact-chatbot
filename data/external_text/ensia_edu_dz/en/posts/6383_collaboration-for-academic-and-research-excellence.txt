[Title: Collaboration for Academic and Research Excellence]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-25T09:08:31]

This evening, November 24, 2024, at the premises of the National Higher School of Commerce in Kolea, a partnership agreement was signed between the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence and the National Higher School of Commerce. The purpose of this agreement is to strengthen collaboration and develop partnerships in all areas of mutual interest, such as scientific research, training, organizing conferences, and supporting students.
