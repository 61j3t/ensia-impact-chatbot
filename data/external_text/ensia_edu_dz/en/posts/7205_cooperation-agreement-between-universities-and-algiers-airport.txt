[Title: Cooperation Agreement Between Universities and Algiers Airport]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-29T09:41:22]

The Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, Mr. Kamel Baddari, presided today, June 4, 2024, over the signing ceremony of a scientific and technological cooperation agreement between three academic institutions and Algiers Airport. The event was attended by the CEO of the company managing the operations and facilities of Algiers Airport, Mr. Mokhtar Mediouni.

The three academic institutions involved are:

Houari Boumediene University of Science and Technology,

The National School of  Mathematics,

The National School of Artificial Intelligence.
