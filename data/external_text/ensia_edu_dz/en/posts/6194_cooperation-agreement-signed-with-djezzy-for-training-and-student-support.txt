[Title: Cooperation Agreement Signature with Djezzy]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-08T09:40:12]

On December 17, 2024, a cooperation agreement was signed with Djezzy, a company specialized in telecommunications and mobile phones. This agreement aims to:

Provide training and internships.

Organize scientific events.

Support students.
