National Higher School for Artificial Intelligence

Algeria: February 02, 2025

The National Higher School for Artificial Intelligence in Sidi Abdallah announces job openings for the following positions :

Application file must contain the following documents:

Written request to participate in the competition

Copy of valid national ID card

Copy of qualification or relevant degree, with transcript of academic record

Application form filled out by the candidate (available for download on the electronic websites of the General Directorate for Public Service and Administrative Reform:
www.concours-fonction-publique.gov.dz
)

Copy of certificate proving the candidate’s status regarding national service

Work certificates proving experience acquired by the candidate in the specialty, accompanied by affiliation certificate issued by the social security authority for professional experience in the private sector, if applicable

Certificates proving work period performed by the candidate under special employment contracts, as a contract worker, if applicable

Document proving higher education than the required degree or certificate for participation in the competition in the same specialty, if applicable

Document proving completed work or studies by the candidate in the same specialty, if applicable

Document proving the candidate’s excellence in studies, if applicable

Copy of the candidate’s disability card, if applicable

Successful candidates must complete their files with the following documents:

Certificate of residence

Extract from birth certificate

Two medical certificates (general medicine and chest diseases issued by a specialist physician) confirming the candidate’s eligibility for the position

Two (2) photographs

Family certificate for married candidates

Applications must be submitted within 15 working days from the first publication date of this announcement at: National Higher School for Artificial Intelligence, headquarters: Electronic Technology Center, Abdelhafid Boussouf, Sidi Abdallah, Algeria.
