[Title: Visit of Rwandan Minister to AI School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-06-02T08:23:47]

On the evening of Sunday, June 1, 2025, the National School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of receiving Ms. Paula Ingabire, Minister of Information and Communication Technology and Innovation of the Republic of Rwanda, as part of her official visit to Algeria.
The school’s director gave a detailed presentation covering the institution, its academic and educational activities, its research and scientific projects, as well as its local and international partnerships in the field of artificial intelligence and higher education.
