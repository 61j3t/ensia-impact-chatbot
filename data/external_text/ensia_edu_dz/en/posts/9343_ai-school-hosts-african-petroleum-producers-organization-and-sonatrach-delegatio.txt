[Title: AI School Hosts African Petroleum Producers Organization and Sonatrach Delegation]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-04-21T16:18:32]

On April 20, 2025, the National School of Artificial Intelligence welcomed Mr. Omar Farouk Ibrahim, Secretary General of the African Petroleum Producers Organization, along with his accompanying delegation of officials from the organization’s research and development centers, as well as representatives from Sonatrach. During the visit, the school’s activities and its collaboration model with Sonatrach were presented.
