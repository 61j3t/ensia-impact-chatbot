[Title: Commemoration of National Martyrs’ Day]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-02T09:13:10]

In commemoration of the National Martyrs’ Day, which falls on February 18th each year, the Director of the National School of Artificial Intelligence, along with the members of the school community, including students and staff, observed a moment of silence in honor of their pure souls and to remember their sacrifices for the country.
