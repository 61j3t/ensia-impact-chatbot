[Title: Dr. Elias Zerhouni Visits AI National School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-29T09:46:14]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence had the honor of receiving a visit from Dr. Elias Zerhouni, the former director of the National Institutes of Health in the United States. During his visit, he explored the school’s facilities, programs, and projects. He also engaged in discussions with the school’s professors and students about technical topics related to the use of artificial intelligence in image processing, computer vision, and their applications in the medical field.
