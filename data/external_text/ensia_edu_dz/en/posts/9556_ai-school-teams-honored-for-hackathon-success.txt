[Title: AI School Teams Honored for Hackathon Success]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-05-11T09:41:50]

On May 10, 2025, the names of the top three winning teams in the hackathon organized last February by the Ministry of Knowledge Economy, Startups, and Small Enterprises were announced. These teams, affiliated with the National School of Artificial Intelligence, were recognized during the first edition of the National Self-Entrepreneurship Exhibition held at the Moufdi Zakaria Palace of Culture.
