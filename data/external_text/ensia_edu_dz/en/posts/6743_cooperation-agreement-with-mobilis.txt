[Title: Cooperation Agreement With Mobilis]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-26T09:20:36]

On October 2, 2024, at the headquarters of Mobilis, a partnership agreement was signed between the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence and Mobilis. This agreement aims to:

Support innovative projects,

Provide training and internships,

Organize scientific events,

Facilitate employment and guidance.
