[Title: Visit of the Tunisian Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research to the School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-04-16T08:24:08]

On April 15, 2025, the National School of Artificial Intelligence had the opportunity to host Mr. Mondher BelAid, Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research of the Republic of Tunisia. He was accompanied by the Ambassador of the Republic of Tunisia to Algeria, along with officials from his country. The delegation was received on the Algerian side by Mr. Abdelhakim Ben Talis, Secretary General of the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, along with a group of officials from the ministry.
The purpose of the visit was to learn about the Sidi Abdallah Scientific and Technological Hub in general, the school’s activities in the field of artificial intelligence, and to discuss ways of cooperation between Tunisian and Algerian academic institutions in the fields of higher education and scientific research.
