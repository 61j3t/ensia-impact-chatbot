[Title: Agreement Signed Between AI School and Algeria Post]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-31T10:34:28]

On April 15, 2024, a framework agreement was signed between the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence and Algeria Post, aiming to:

Support projects

Training and internships

Organize scientific events

Recruitment and support
