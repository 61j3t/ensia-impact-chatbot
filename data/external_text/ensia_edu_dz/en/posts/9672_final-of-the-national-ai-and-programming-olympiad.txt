[Title: Final of the National AI and Programming Olympiad]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-05-18T09:11:03]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence hosted yesterday, May 15, 2025, the final of the National Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence and Programming. In its first edition, the Olympiad went through three stages, starting with local-level qualifiers, followed by regional and finally national-level competitions. Best wishes to all the participating student teams.
