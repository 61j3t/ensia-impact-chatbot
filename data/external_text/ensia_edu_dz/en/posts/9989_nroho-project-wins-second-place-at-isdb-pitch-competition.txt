[Title: Nroho Project Wins Second Place at IsDB Pitch Competition]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-05-27T10:32:55]

The Nroho project, developed by a team of students from the National School of Artificial Intelligence, achieved second place in the IsDB Pitch Competition organized by the Islamic Development Bank, in the category of sustainability and food security, during the 2025 annual meetings held in Algeria.
This recognition came after a strong competition with more than 40 national startups.
The school congratulates the student team on this achievement and wishes the Nroho team continued success.
