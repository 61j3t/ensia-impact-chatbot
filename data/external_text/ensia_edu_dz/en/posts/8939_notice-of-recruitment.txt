[Title: NOTICE OF RECRUITMENT]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-03-03T16:11:44]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence is organizing a recruitment competition for the following positions.

Positions Available

Application File Requirements

The application file must contain the following documents:

A handwritten application

A copy of national identity card

A copy of the required diploma or qualification, or recognized equivalent; and a copy of academic transcript

An information form completed by the candidate (downloaded from the following website:
www.concours-fonction-publique.gov.dz
)

A document certifying the candidate’s status regarding national service

A certificate justifying the work period completed by the candidate under the professional or social insertion program for graduates, as a contractor, if applicable

Work certificates justifying the candidate’s professional experience in the specialty; these must be accompanied by certificates from the social security organization for experience acquired in the private sector

A certificate justifying the completion of additional higher education training to the required qualification or diploma for participation in the competition in the same specialty, if applicable

A document justifying work or studies completed by the candidate in the specialty, if applicable

A certificate justifying that the candidate is a major contender for promotion, if applicable

A copy of the disability card of the candidate, if applicable

Additional Documents Required After Admission

Candidates who are definitively admitted must, prior to their appointment to the position, complete their application file with the following documents:

A certificate of residence

A birth certificate extract

Two medical certificates (general medicine and phthisiology) issued by a specialist doctor, attesting to the candidate’s fitness for the position

Two identity photos

Note
: Application files must be sent or submitted to the following address: National School of Artificial Intelligence, The Scientific and Technological Hub of the University, martyr Abdelhafid Ihaddaden, Sidi Abdellah, Mahelma Alger within 15 days from the date of the first publication of this notice.

Any incomplete file or received after the deadline will not be considered.
