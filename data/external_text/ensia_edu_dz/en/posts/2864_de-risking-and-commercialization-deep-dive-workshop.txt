[Title: “De-Risking and Commercialization Deep Dive” Workshop]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-03-07T13:53:24]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence hosted the workshop “De-Risking and Commercialization Deep Dive” which lasted for four days, from Sunday, Feb. 14 to Wednesday, Feb. 15. The workshop was delivered by a group of researchers from The IDEA Center, Notre Dame University (USA). This was a collaborative event that involved our school, Notre Dame University, The National School of Mathematics (NHSM) and The Center for Development of Advanced Technologies (CDTA), as part of the Algerian Research Hub project.
