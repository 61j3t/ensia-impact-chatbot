[Title: Turkish Delegation Visits AI National School]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-15T09:09:38]

On January 14, 2025, a delegation from the “Turkish Presidency Abroad and Related Communities” visited the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence. The head of the delegation provided explanations about the organization’s activities. In turn, the school’s director gave an overview of the institution and its collaborative activities at the national and international levels.
