[Title: Bridging Education and Business]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-30T10:54:35]

As part of connecting the school with economic institutions and enabling students to access the world of business and entrepreneurship while gaining practical experience, workshops, lectures, and job interviews were organized over three days with the participation of major companies, small businesses, and startups from both the public and private sectors.
