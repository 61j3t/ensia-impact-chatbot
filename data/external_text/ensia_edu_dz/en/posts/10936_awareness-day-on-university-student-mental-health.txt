[Title: Awareness Day on University Student Mental Health]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-11-16T11:40:27]

On November 12, 2025, the National School of Artificial Intelligence, in coordination with the Samia Ben Wanish University Psychological Assistance Center at University of Algiers 2, directed by Professor Nadia Baddad together with the school’s psychological counselor, Professor Sajia Makhlouf, organized an awareness day on the mental health of university students.
