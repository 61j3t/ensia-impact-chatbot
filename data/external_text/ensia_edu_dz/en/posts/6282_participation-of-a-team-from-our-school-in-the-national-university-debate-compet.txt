[Title: School Team Competes in National University Debate Competition]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2025-01-14T09:32:47]

A team from the National School of Artificial Intelligence participated in the third edition of the National University Debate Competition at Amar Telidji University in Laghouat, where student Ziad Kherrat secured second place among the top ten debaters.
