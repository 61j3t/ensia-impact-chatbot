[Title: Launch of the “Knowledge Day” Talk Series]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2023-03-07T13:54:02]

The National School of Artificial Intelligence announced the launch of the talk series named “The Knowledge Day” on Tuesday, Feb. 14. The presence of the two speakers Dr. Hamid MENOUAR and Mr. Mourad DELHOUM, marked the first edition of this series. They respectively gave talks titled “Falcon-I: AI Based Platform for Enabling Smart Spaces for Smart and Cognitive Cities” and “Storage Technology: State of the Art”.
