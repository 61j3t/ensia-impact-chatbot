[Title: Students Present Projects to Business Leaders]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2024-12-29T09:22:38]

On Thursday, June 6, 2024, third-year students met with business leaders and startup founders to evaluate their projects as part of the courses “Group Project” and “Mobile Development.”
The event began with a presentation by Mr. Noureddine Wadih, the Director of Startups and Incubators.
