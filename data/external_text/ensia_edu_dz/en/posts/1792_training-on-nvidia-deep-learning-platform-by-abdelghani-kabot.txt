[Title: Workshop on Nvidia Deep Learning platform by A. Kabot]
[Language: en]
[Modified: 2022-05-26T16:12:51]

A training session on NVIDIA’s Deep Learning platform was given by Mr Abdelghan Kabot, a certified trainer at NVIDEA.

The objectives of the course are:

Learn the fundamental techniques and tools needed to understand a deep learning model.
Gain experience with the most common deep learning data types and model architectures.
Improvement of datasets through data augmentation to improve model accuracy.
Take advantage of learning transfer between models to achieve efficient results with less data and computation.
Gain the confidence to undertake a project with a modern deep learning framework.
