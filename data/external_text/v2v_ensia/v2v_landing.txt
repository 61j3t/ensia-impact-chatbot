AI-First Incubator

Every workshop, resource, and mentorship session is designed around artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven product development. Our students don't just learn theory — they build real AI models, ship intelligent products, and solve problems that matter.

STARTUP TEAM

Nroho

Nroho is an Algerian mobility company developing a smart, community-driven transport ecosystem designed for Africa and beyond. The company delivers solutions that make travel more affordable, secure, and comfortable, while improving the overall user experience. Its ecosystem includes consumer carpooling, enterprise mobility solutions, transport management software, and a hybrid VTC offering, allowing Nroho to serve both individual users and businesses through a unified mobility platform.
