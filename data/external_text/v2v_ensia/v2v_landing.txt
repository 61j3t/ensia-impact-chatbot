Frequently Asked Questions

01. What is V2V?
V2V is ENSiA's AI-focused incubator that helps students turn their AI ideas into concrete, impactful projects.

02. Who can apply to the incubator?
Any student or team of students with innovative AI ideas and a passion for solving real-world problems can apply. We welcome participants from all academic backgrounds and skill levels.

03. What support do selected teams receive?
Selected teams receive mentorship from industry experts, access to resources and tools, networking opportunities, workspace, and guidance throughout their development journey.

04. How does the selection process work?
Applications are reviewed by our expert panel based on innovation, feasibility, impact potential, and team capability. Shortlisted teams are invited for interviews before final selection.

05. How are startups evaluated?
Startups are evaluated on multiple criteria including innovation, market potential, technical feasibility, team composition, social impact, and scalability of the solution.

06. Do I need a complete product to apply?
No, you don't need a complete product. We accept applications at the idea stage, but having a prototype or MVP demonstrates commitment and increases your chances of selection.
